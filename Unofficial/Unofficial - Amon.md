# Unofficial - Amon

**Status (2026-10-07):** there is one worked-out theory I could read, and it lives on a wiki: the Tormentpedia's *The Gods* page argues that "Amon" is an unnamed sun god opposed to Rher. Its evidence is checked below; most of the individual facts hold, but the conclusion is the wiki's inference, not something any game text says. Reddit and the videos where this is likely argued at length could not be read (see [[Unofficial - Source log]]).

## 1. Starting facts [WIKI-REPORTED]
These are what the theories begin from. They are the wikis' statements, not theory.

- The name on the museum clock. Main wiki, *The Gods*, trivia (revision 49336, 2026-09-05): "There are three unofficially named gods whose names are present in the game files on the clock in the Museum, but not mentioned in-game. Their names are Amon for the first sigil, Turkil for the seventh, and Sarko for the tenth, respectively. The name Amon shares the same name as the City of the Sun in Abyssonia" — <https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Gods>
- The place. Main wiki, *Abyssonia* (revision 48690, 2026-05-31): "…the City of the Sun, Amon. This city is renowned for being a center of education for the mind and religion for its inhabitants. At the heart of Amon lies the Temple of Amon…" — <https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Abyssonia>
- Where the first sigil appears. Main wiki, *The Gods* (same revision): "The first sigil in the museum clock also appears in the Book of enlightenment and in the Heartless One's Curse of Light spell." Main wiki, *Book of enlightenment* (revision 47771, 2026-01-31): "The 1st god sigil in the museum clock is found in the right hand-side of this book." — <https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Book_of_enlightenment>

## 2. The sun-god theory [THEORY, hosted on the Tormentpedia]
The Tormentpedia (a separate community wiki), *The Gods*, section "Unnamed Sun God (Amon)" (revision 16519, 2026-06-11) — <https://fearandhunger.fandom.com/wiki/The_Gods>. The section in full, as written there (spelling as it appears):

> "According to the Skin Bible - Rher, the sun is also a deity, likely oppositional to Rher, given the existence of the happy mask within the Golden Gates. Another piece of evidence towards the existence of such a sun god is The Heartless One's attacks, spesifically Curse Of Light and Pillar Of Light, which use sigils from the Book of Enlightenment. One of those symbols appear on the Museum clock, which in datamined files is labled Amon. These point toward an idea of 'Amon', being a god of the sun and light, oppositional to Rher."

## 3. A cut book that names Amon [WIKI-REPORTED, weak evidence]
The Tormentpedia transcribes a book called *Seildna Modnaf Dilemna* from the files of the first game. Page revision 16341 (2025-08-24): <https://fearandhunger.fandom.com/wiki/Seildna_Modnaf_Dilemna>

The passage that mentions Amon, exactly as that page prints it:

> "With its Old God master Amon long gone theres barely anything stopping it from trying to take the knowledge this species has gathered over milennia and warp it beyond our wildest thoughts."

(The "it" is "the Lesser God" in the preceding sentence of the book.)

**Cautions printed on the same page:** the book "laid dormant in the files of Fear & Hunger until the 17th of August 2023" and "is speculated to be a part of cut content"; "It is unused in the main game"; "The Chinese characters above the image of the deity reads: 'this is random nonsense'"; and "Seildna Modnaf backwards reads: 'Fandom and lies'". Those last two notes suggest (my inference, not the page's statement) that the book may be a joke or filler file, so it should not be treated as lore. The same page also notes that Amon "is a name assigned to a symbol on the Prehevil Museum clock … implying a connection."

## 4. A "sun god" line in unused game text [WIKI-REPORTED, unused]
Main wiki, *Per'kele/Dialogue* (revision 46903, 2025-09-12), under the heading "Unused" → "perkele_discuss Common Event" — <https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Per%27kele/Dialogue>. The wiki lists this under "Unused", meaning it is not part of the played game.

> Per'kele: "Since the dawn of men you've tried to please the sun god to keep you warm and give good harvest..."
> Per'kele: "You've worshipped destruction and creation, in the delusion that you'd have say in those matters..."
> Per'kele: "They are not there to help you, to harm you or to be worshipped by you. They just exist in the greater scheme of things."

It speaks of humans worshipping a sun god in general terms and does not name Amon.
