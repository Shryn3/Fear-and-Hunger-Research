# 5e - Soul - Endless

**Contestants in the wiki's tables:** Cahara, Karin

> [!tip] Suggested 5e shape [DESIGN SUGGESTION]
> A fugitive/negotiator package: Thieves' Tools, Dash and Disengage, Influence checks, Alert-style initiative.
> This line is the author's design suggestion, not text from any book.

## Skill by skill

Wiki text is quoted from the Fear & Hunger wiki's skill lists (https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H2 and https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H1). Fit: 🟢 Close, 🟡 Partial, ⚪ Loose, none = no SRD comparable found. Fit notes are the author's judgment; the passages themselves are in [[5e - SRD passages]].

> [!hexen]- Lockpicking (F&H1 · Soul skills · Cahara)
>
> **Wiki description:** An ability to open any simple lock with your trusty lockpick.
>
> **Effect:** Allows you to open doors with simple locks.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Thieves' Tools|Thieves' Tools]] (tool, SRD p. 94). Pick a lock (DC 15).
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Knock|Knock]] (spell, SRD p. 143). Unlocks by magic rather than skill.

> [!hexen]- Steal (F&H1 · Soul skills · Cahara)
>
> **Wiki description:** Steal from the rich and give to yourself.
>
> **Effect:** Obtain items from enemies during the battle.
>
> **Cost:** Free
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Thieves' Tools|Thieves' Tools]] (tool, SRD p. 94). A tool kit for thieves' tasks; the SRD has no rule for taking items from a creature in combat.

> [!hexen]- En garde (F&H1 · Soul skills · Cahara)
>
> **Wiki description:** Long combat experience has prepared you for fights. Prepare for an attack to gain advantage with [SHIFT].
>
> **Effect:** Using [SHIFT] before engaging in battle gives you an extra turn on the first move.
>
> **Cost:** Free
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Alert (feat)|Alert (feat)]] (feat, SRD p. 87). Adds Proficiency Bonus to Initiative and allows an Initiative swap with a willing ally.

> [!hexen]- Dash (F&H1 · Soul skills · Cahara)
>
> **Wiki description:** Your movement speed increases greatly. Running can prove out to be a really valuable skill.
>
> **Effect:** Increases player's base speed in the overworld.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Dash (Action)|Dash (Action)]] (rules glossary, SRD p. 180). Gains extra movement for the turn.
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Longstrider|Longstrider]] (spell, SRD p. 145). Raises a creature's Speed by 10 feet for an hour.

> [!hexen]- Escape plan (F&H1 · General skills · Cahara)
>
> **Wiki description:** A skill that greatly enhances your chances for running away from fights.
>
> **Effect:** Increases the chance of running away from the battle when using **Run** skill.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **Unlock:** Cahara starting choice
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Disengage (Action)|Disengage (Action)]] (rules glossary, SRD p. 181). Movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks; the SRD has no 'chance to flee combat' rule.

> [!hexen]- Persuade (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Karin)
>
> **Wiki description:** Persuade your opponents with greater intellect and wit. The effects vary between enemy to enemy depending on their persona. Used with Talk skill.
>
> **Effect:** Unlocks [PERSUADE] option in battle dialogues when using **Talk** skill.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Influence (Action)|Influence (Action)]] (rules glossary, SRD p. 184). Gentle persuasion is one of the ways the Influence action describes urging a monster.

> [!hexen]- Lockpicking (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Karin)
>
> **Wiki description:** An ability to open any simple lock with your trusty lockpick.
>
> **Effect:** Allows you to open doors with simple locks.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Thieves' Tools|Thieves' Tools]] (tool, SRD p. 94). Pick a lock (DC 15).
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Knock|Knock]] (spell, SRD p. 143). Unlocks by magic rather than skill.

> [!hexen]- Escape plan (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Karin)
>
> **Wiki description:** A skill that greatly enhances your chances of running away from fights. Planning for an escape in advance pays off. A passive skill.
>
> **Effect:** Doubles the chance of running away from the battle when using **Run** skill.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Disengage (Action)|Disengage (Action)]] (rules glossary, SRD p. 181). Movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks; the SRD has no 'chance to flee combat' rule.

> [!hexen]- Diplomacy (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Karin)
>
> **Wiki description:** Start the fight by talking to the opponent without wasting a turn. Maybe you can find a diplomatic resolution before any violence takes place?
>
> **Effect:** Triggers **Talk** skill at the start of the battle without wasting a turn.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Influence (Action)|Influence (Action)]] (rules glossary, SRD p. 184). The Influence action; the SRD makes it an action, while Diplomacy triggers Talk for free at the start of battle.

> [!hexen]- Dash (F&H2 · Unused skills · Karin)
>
> **Unlock:** Karin (Endless soul)
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Dash (Action)|Dash (Action)]] (rules glossary, SRD p. 180). Gains extra movement for the turn.
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Longstrider|Longstrider]] (spell, SRD p. 145). Raises a creature's Speed by 10 feet for an hour.
