# 5e - Soul - Enlightened

**Contestants in the wiki's tables:** Enki, Marina, O'saa

> [!tip] Suggested 5e shape [DESIGN SUGGESTION]
> A caster package: Counterspell/Dispel Magic, divination spells, and Metamagic-style modification of spells fuelled by a point pool.
> This line is the author's design suggestion, not text from any book.

## Skill by skill

Wiki text is quoted from the Fear & Hunger wiki's skill lists (https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H2 and https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H1). Fit: 🟢 Close, 🟡 Partial, ⚪ Loose, none = no SRD comparable found. Fit notes are the author's judgment; the passages themselves are in [[5e - SRD passages]].

> [!hexen]- Counter-magic (F&H1 · Soul skills · Enki)
>
> **Wiki description:** Undo the effects of any lesser magic by your superior knowledge in the greater scheme of things.
>
> **Effect:** Resists or mends the effects of magical attacks. Can be used to break seals like the Purifying talisman.
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Counterspell|Counterspell]] (spell, SRD p. 120). Interrupts a spell as a Reaction.
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Dispel Magic|Dispel Magic]] (spell, SRD p. 124). Ends ongoing magical effects, which matches breaking seals.

> [!hexen]- Greater blood magic (F&H1 · Soul skills · Enki)
>
> **Wiki description:** Years spent studying blood magic has given you an ability to use magic more efficiently.
>
> **Effect:** Increases the potential of blood magic, enabling the use of greater versions of Hurting, Necromancy and Blood golem.
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - none: no SRD 5.2.1 passage found that covers this skill.

> [!hexen]- La Danse Macabre (F&H2 · Contestant skills · O'saa)
>
> **Wiki description:** A ritual dance used to channel otherwordly powers to be used by the performer. Raises otherwordly powers gradually over the course of a fight. A passive skill.
>
> **Effect:** Increases M.Attack at the end of each turn for 3 turns: - Turn 1 - ×150% M.Attack - Turn 2 - ×270% M.Attack - Turn 3 - ×500% M.Attack
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - none: no SRD 5.2.1 passage found that covers this skill.

> [!hexen]- Meditation (F&H2 · Contestant skills · O'saa)
>
> **Wiki description:** Reach a higher state of awareness. Meditate while guarding to gain extra Rev points on that turn.
>
> **Effect:** Guarding grants +2 Rev points at the end of the turn. ⚠ *(known bug listed on the wiki)*
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Font of Magic (Sorcerer)|Font of Magic (Sorcerer)]] (class feature, SRD p. 66). A pool of points that fuels other features; Rev points are a similar idea.

> [!hexen]- Greater meditation (F&H2 · Contestant skills · O'saa)
>
> **Wiki description:** Reach a greater state of awareness. Meditate while guarding to gain even more Rev points on that turn.
>
> **Effect:** No effect. ⚠ *(known bug listed on the wiki)*
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - none: no SRD 5.2.1 passage found that covers this skill.

> [!hexen]- Spice forge (F&H2 · Contestant skills · O'saa)
>
> **Wiki description:** Spice forges were widely used by magick practitioners of the Middle East back in the day. Spices make it possible to customize the properties of your otherwordly skills.
>
> **Effect:** Give spells special effects. First (White) magic happens at the start of battle; Second (Red) magic casts a spell twice at once but hits a random target; Greater (Blue) magic costs less Mind.
>
> **Cost:** Free
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Metamagic (Sorcerer)|Metamagic (Sorcerer)]] (class feature, SRD p. 66). Alter a spell as you cast it by spending points.
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Quickened Spell|Quickened Spell]] (class feature option, SRD p. 67). Changes casting time.
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Twinned Spell|Twinned Spell]] (class feature option, SRD p. 67). Affects an additional target of a spell.

> [!hexen]- Premonitions (F&H2 · Unused skills · Marina)
>
> **Effect:** Shows images displaying some potential important events during the current time and day
>
> **Unlock:** Marina (Enlightened soul) (Demo)
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Augury|Augury]] (spell, SRD p. 111). Gives an omen about the results of a planned course of action.
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Divination|Divination]] (spell, SRD p. 125). Answers a question about events within 7 days.

> [!hexen]- Third eye (F&H2 · Unused skills · Marina)
>
> **Wiki description:** Awaken your inner third eye - A deep form of enlightenment. Greatly raise your Rev Point regeneration per turns.
>
> **Effect:** Grants max Rev points at the end of each turn.
>
> **Unlock:** Marina (Enlightened soul) (Demo)
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - none: no SRD 5.2.1 passage found that covers this skill.
