# 5e - Soul - Shadowed

**Contestants in the wiki's tables:** Olivia

> [!tip] Suggested 5e shape [DESIGN SUGGESTION]
> A herbalist-poisoner package: Herbalism Kit, Poisoner's Kit, Basic Poison.
> This line is the author's design suggestion, not text from any book.

## Skill by skill

Wiki text is quoted from the Fear & Hunger wiki's skill lists (https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H2 and https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H1). Fit: 🟢 Close, 🟡 Partial, ⚪ Loose, none = no SRD comparable found. Fit notes are the author's judgment; the passages themselves are in [[5e - SRD passages]].

> [!hexen]- Advanced botanism (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Olivia)
>
> **Wiki description:** The skill to condense the effective properties of herbs and plants to maximize their potential. Teaches you the recipes for condensed blue and green herbs as well as lavender.
>
> **Effect:** Unlocks recipes for: - Mixed herbs (dark blue, red) - Mixed herbs (dark blue, blue) - Condensed blue - Condensed green - Condensed lavender
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Herbalism Kit|Herbalism Kit]] (tool, SRD p. 94). Identify a plant; crafts Antitoxin, Candle, Healer's Kit and Potion of Healing.

> [!hexen]- Poison tip (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Olivia)
>
> **Wiki description:** Coat your weapon in poison extracted from the local plantlife. The poison tip takes a small toll on your sanity because of the constant stress of handling a poison coated weapon.
>
> **Effect:** Applies Poison Tip status, can be used before battle.
>
> **Cost:** 5 Mind
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Poison, Basic|Poison, Basic]] (adventuring gear, SRD p. 99). Coat a weapon for extra Poison damage.
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Poisoner's Kit|Poisoner's Kit]] (tool, SRD p. 94). Crafts Basic Poison.

> [!hexen]- Undergrowth awareness (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Olivia)
>
> **Wiki description:** You can harvest irritable and poisonous plants safely and also tell apart the plants that have potential use in your situation. A passive skill useful on the field.
>
> **Effect:** Gives an option to harvest Dark blue root, Poison hemlock, Nettle and Wild dagga in the overworld.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Herbalism Kit|Herbalism Kit]] (tool, SRD p. 94). Identify a plant (DC 10); harvesting has no SRD rule.

> [!hexen]- Toxicology (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Olivia)
>
> **Wiki description:** The skill to condense the effective properties of different poisonous plants to maximize their potential. Teaches you the recipes for condensed nettle and condensed hemlock.
>
> **Effect:** Unlocks recipes for: - Condensed nettle - Condensed hemlock
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Poisoner's Kit|Poisoner's Kit]] (tool, SRD p. 94). Tool kit for making poisons.
