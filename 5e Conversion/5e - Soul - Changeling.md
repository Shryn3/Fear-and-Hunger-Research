# 5e - Soul - Changeling

**Contestants in the wiki's tables:** Marina

> [!tip] Suggested 5e shape [DESIGN SUGGESTION]
> A warded-sigil package: a Glyph of Warding style mark plus a small resource pool in the manner of Sorcery Points.
> This line is the author's design suggestion, not text from any book.

## Skill by skill

Wiki text is quoted from the Fear & Hunger wiki's skill lists (https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H2 and https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H1). Fit: 🟢 Close, 🟡 Partial, ⚪ Loose, none = no SRD comparable found. Fit notes are the author's judgment; the passages themselves are in [[5e - SRD passages]].

> [!hexen]- Engrave (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Marina)
>
> **Wiki description:** Engrave a sigil on a party member. The benefit of the sigil varies depeding on the god it belongs to. Historically sigils were carved on the body, but modern practition prefers face sigils.
>
> **Effect:** Allows you to carve a sigil on your face to gain a certain buff: - Gro-goroth - +4 Attack - Sylvian - +3 M.Defense, ×70% Otherworldly damage received - Alll-mer - +3 Defense, ×85% Physical damage received - Rher - +25 Max Mind - Vinushka - +4 M.Attack - God of Fear and Hunger - +5 Agility
>
> **Cost:** Free
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Bless|Bless]] (spell, SRD p. 113). A temporary, visible-on-the-target buff granted by a mark or blessing; the god-specific buffs have no SRD equivalent.

> [!hexen]- Warding sigil (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Marina)
>
> **Wiki description:** Draw a warding sigil on the ground to fend off malice and creatures with ill intent. Originally created by the Virgin Mages of the Eastern Sanctuaries.
>
> **Effect:** Allows you to create a sigil on the tile you're currently standing on which will block enemies from walking on that tile, expires on map transition.
>
> **Cost:** 10 Mind
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Glyph of Warding|Glyph of Warding]] (spell, SRD p. 136). Inscribes a glyph on a surface that later has an effect.
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Alarm|Alarm]] (spell, SRD p. 107). Wards an area against creatures that enter it.

> [!hexen]- Advanced occultism (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Marina)
>
> **Wiki description:** Your advanced knowledge in occult arts enables you to start a battle with an extra Rev point. A passive skill.
>
> **Effect:** Gives +1 Rev point at the start of the battle.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Font of Magic (Sorcerer)|Font of Magic (Sorcerer)]] (class feature, SRD p. 66). A pool of points (Sorcery Points) that fuels other features; Rev points are a similar idea.

> [!hexen]- Greater occultism (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Marina)
>
> **Wiki description:** Your greater knowledge in occult arts enables you to start a battle with 2 extra Rev points. A passive skill.
>
> **Effect:** Gives +1 Rev point at the start of the battle.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Font of Magic (Sorcerer)|Font of Magic (Sorcerer)]] (class feature, SRD p. 66). A pool of points (Sorcery Points) that fuels other features; Rev points are a similar idea.
