# 5e - Soul - Decrepit

**Contestants in the wiki's tables:** Caligura

> [!tip] Suggested 5e shape [DESIGN SUGGESTION]
> A scavenger package: Influence-style intimidation, tool use, thrown explosives such as Alchemist's Fire.
> This line is the author's design suggestion, not text from any book.

## Skill by skill

Wiki text is quoted from the Fear & Hunger wiki's skill lists (https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H2 and https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H1). Fit: 🟢 Close, 🟡 Partial, ⚪ Loose, none = no SRD comparable found. Fit notes are the author's judgment; the passages themselves are in [[5e - SRD passages]].

> [!hexen]- Steal (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Caligura)
>
> **Wiki description:** Try to pickpocket goods from your opponents. You never know what you might find.
>
> **Effect:** Use in battle to obtain items from enemies.
>
> **Cost:** Free
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Thieves' Tools|Thieves' Tools]] (tool, SRD p. 94). A tool kit for thieves' tasks; the SRD has no rule for taking items from a creature in combat.

> [!hexen]- Intimidate (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Caligura)
>
> **Wiki description:** Intimidation tactics in battle work on those mentally weak. The effects vary case by case. Used while initiating Talk during combat.
>
> **Effect:** Unlocks [INTIMIDATE] option in battle dialogues when using **Talk** skill.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Influence (Action)|Influence (Action)]] (rules glossary, SRD p. 184). The Influence action covers urging a monster by intimidating it.

> [!hexen]- Killing intent (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Caligura)
>
> **Wiki description:** You killing intent eminates strong. This will scare away the weaker opponent so you don't need to bother with them.
>
> **Effect:** The following enemies will start avoiding you in the overworld: - Villager (Sickle) - Villager (Knives) - Fecal hound - Fallen cherub (Only the solo cherubs)
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Frightened (Condition)|Frightened (Condition)]] (rules glossary, SRD p. 182). The Frightened condition stops a creature moving closer to the source of fear.
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Fear|Fear]] (spell, SRD p. 130). Makes creatures in a cone drop what they hold and run away.

> [!hexen]- Explosives (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Caligura)
>
> **Wiki description:** Getting creative in a wartorn times is a must. Craft explosives to be used in combat from simple everyday ingredients.
>
> **Effect:** Unlocks a recipe for: - Pipe bomb
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Alchemist's Fire|Alchemist's Fire]] (adventuring gear, SRD p. 94). A thrown flask that deals Fire damage.
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Alchemist's Supplies|Alchemist's Supplies]] (tool, SRD p. 93). Tool kit that crafts Alchemist's Fire.

> [!hexen]- Bribe (F&H2 · Unused skills · Caligura)
>
> **Unlock:** Caligura (Decrepit soul)
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - none: no SRD 5.2.1 passage found that covers this skill.
