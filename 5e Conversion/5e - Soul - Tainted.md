# 5e - Soul - Tainted

**Contestants in the wiki's tables:** Marcoh

> [!tip] Suggested 5e shape [DESIGN SUGGESTION]
> A martial-artist package: Unarmed Strike and Martial Arts, Dodge, Deflect Attacks, Flurry of Blows, Haste.
> This line is the author's design suggestion, not text from any book.

## Skill by skill

Wiki text is quoted from the Fear & Hunger wiki's skill lists (https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H2 and https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H1). Fit: 🟢 Close, 🟡 Partial, ⚪ Loose, none = no SRD comparable found. Fit notes are the author's judgment; the passages themselves are in [[5e - SRD passages]].

> [!hexen]- Bare-fisted proficiency (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Marcoh)
>
> **Wiki description:** Proficiency with your bare fists gives you greater accuracy when fighting without a weapon. A passive skill.
>
> **Effect:** Gives +30% Hit Rate when no weapon is equipped in battle.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strike]] (rules glossary, SRD p. 190). The base rules for fighting without a weapon.
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Martial Arts (Monk)|Martial Arts (Monk)]] (class feature, SRD p. 50). Improves Unarmed Strike and Monk weapons.

> [!hexen]- Bob and weave (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Marcoh)
>
> **Wiki description:** A defensive technique where one bobs their head laterally to an incoming attack while shifting balance to either left or right. Raises evasion greatly.
>
> **Effect:** Applies Evasion Buff status.
>
> **Cost:** Free
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Dodge (Action)|Dodge (Action)]] (rules glossary, SRD p. 181). Disadvantage on attacks against you and advantage on Dexterity saves until your next turn.

> [!hexen]- Perfect guard (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Marcoh)
>
> **Wiki description:** While guarding your damage is reduced even further and you can evade the most drastic physical trauma.
>
> **Effect:** Prevents limb loss while guarding until the end of the turn.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Shield|Shield]] (spell, SRD p. 161). A Reaction that raises AC for a turn; it doesn't prevent limb loss, which has no SRD equivalent.

> [!hexen]- Counter stance (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Marcoh)
>
> **Wiki description:** Stay alert for enemy attacks and counter when possible.
>
> **Effect:** Applies Counter status. *Counter stance* does not work with the Lugr Pistol, Rifle .303 Mk I or 12-gauge Trenchgun, but works with the Bayonet
>
> **Cost:** Free
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Deflect Attacks (Monk)|Deflect Attacks (Monk)]] (class feature, SRD p. 51). A Reaction that reduces a hit's damage and can redirect force.
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attacks]] (rules glossary, SRD p. 185). A Reaction attack triggered by a creature leaving your reach.

> [!hexen]- Adrenaline rush (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Marcoh)
>
> **Wiki description:** The longer the combat goes on, the stronger you get as your blood boils ever hotter.
>
> **Effect:** Increases Attack at the end of each turn for 3 turns: - Turn 1 - ×105% Attack - Turn 2 - ×110% Attack - Turn 3 - ×130% Attack
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Haste|Haste]] (spell, SRD p. 139). A temporary combat boost; this skill escalates over three turns, which no SRD feature does.

> [!hexen]- Fast stance (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Marcoh)
>
> **Wiki description:** Raise your speed with swift footwork. In most cases you get and extra turn between enemy attacks.
>
> **Effect:** Applies Speed Buff status.
>
> **Cost:** Free
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Haste|Haste]] (spell, SRD p. 139). Doubles Speed and grants an extra action for the duration.

> [!hexen]- Quick jabs (F&H2 · General skills · Marcoh)
>
> **Effect:** Deals 120-180 (50 + Attack * 4 - 20 (20% variance)) damage twice to one target.
>
> **Cost:** Free
>
> **Success rate:** 100% (Physical Attack)
>
> **Unlock:** Replaces Marcoh's *Attack* skill when bare-handed
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Monk's Focus (Flurry of Blows, Patient Defense, Step of the Wind)|Monk's Focus (Flurry of Blows, Patient Defense, Step of the Wind)]] (class feature, SRD p. 50). Flurry of Blows is two Unarmed Strikes as a Bonus Action.
