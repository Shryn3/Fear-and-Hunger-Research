# 5e - Soul - Blank

**Contestants in the wiki's tables:** Daan

> [!tip] Suggested 5e shape [DESIGN SUGGESTION]
> A medic package: an Origin/General-feat sized bundle built around ending conditions and reviving, in the range of Lesser Restoration and Revivify.
> This line is the author's design suggestion, not text from any book.

## Skill by skill

Wiki text is quoted from the Fear & Hunger wiki's skill lists (https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H2 and https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H1). Fit: 🟢 Close, 🟡 Partial, ⚪ Loose, none = no SRD comparable found. Fit notes are the author's judgment; the passages themselves are in [[5e - SRD passages]].

> [!hexen]- Diagnosis (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Daan)
>
> **Wiki description:** Study fallen enemies and their anatomy to gain knowledge of them. Diagnosis also reveals what strategies might work the best when facing similar foes in the future.
>
> **Effect:** Gives an option to examine defeated enemies in the overworld to find their weaknesses.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Study (Action)|Study (Action)]] (rules glossary, SRD p. 189). Study is an Intelligence check to call a piece of information to mind; the SRD has no rule for revealing a creature's weaknesses from its corpse.

> [!hexen]- Medicinal (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Daan)
>
> **Wiki description:** Sacrifice harvested organs to Vitruvia, the architect of the human body. A sacrifice will cure a corresponding status ailment. Medicinal is based on the law where every action has an equal reaction.
>
> **Effect:** Gives an option to use harvested organs to cure different status effects for 10 mind (Fracture requires only 7 mind): / Liver (Toxic) / Kidney (Poisoned) / Eye (Blindness) / Heart (Bleeding) / Parathyroid glands (Fracture) / Thymus (Infected arm/leg)
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Lesser Restoration|Lesser Restoration]] (spell, SRD p. 144). Ends one condition on a touched creature, from a short list.
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Protection from Poison|Protection from Poison]] (spell, SRD p. 157). Ends the Poisoned condition only.
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Cure Wounds|Cure Wounds]] (spell, SRD p. 121). Restores Hit Points rather than curing a status.

> [!hexen]- Organ harvest (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Daan)
>
> **Wiki description:** Harvest organs from fallen foes to sacrifice them with your medicinal skills. The desecration of bodies used to be a taboo subject, but it is now widely used among medical practitioners.
>
> **Effect:** Gives an option to harvest organs from defeated enemies in the overworld.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - none: no SRD 5.2.1 passage found that covers this skill.

> [!hexen]- Precision stance (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Daan)
>
> **Wiki description:** A special stance to raise your accuracy greatly. Often used during medical surgeries to prevent one's arm from shaking.
>
> **Effect:** Applies Accuracy Boost status.
>
> **Cost:** 5 Mind
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Bless|Bless]] (spell, SRD p. 113). Adds a bonus to attack rolls (and saves) for the duration.
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Archery (feat)|Archery (feat)]] (feat, SRD p. 87). A flat bonus to ranged attack rolls only.

> [!hexen]- Magna-medicinal (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Daan)
>
> **Wiki description:** A greater form of medicinal where the surgeon will sacrifice their own limbs in exhange for human life. The procedure must be performed on the spot during an encounter to work properly.
>
> **Effect:** Gives an option to sacrifice a limb to revive a party member.
>
> **Cost:** 50 Mind / 1 limb
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Revivify|Revivify]] (spell, SRD p. 159). Revives a creature that died within the last minute; it consumes a costly component, not a limb.
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Raise Dead|Raise Dead]] (spell, SRD p. 157). Revives a creature dead up to 10 days; also a consumed diamond.

> [!hexen]- Analyze (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Daan)
>
> **Wiki description:** Study the anatomy of your opponent to quickly find the biggest weaknesses it their physique.
>
> **Effect:** Inflicts Weakness status to target's head.
>
> **Cost:** 20 Mind
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Hunter's Mark|Hunter's Mark]] (spell, SRD p. 141). Marks a creature as quarry for extra damage.
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Vulnerability|Vulnerability]] (rules glossary, SRD p. 191). Defines Vulnerability, which doubles damage of a type; Analyze applies a Weakness status instead.

> [!hexen]- Greater Analyze (F&H2 · Unused skills · Daan)

>
> **Unlock:** Daan (Blank soul)
