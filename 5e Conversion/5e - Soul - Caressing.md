# 5e - Soul - Caressing

**Contestants in the wiki's tables:** Abella

> [!tip] Suggested 5e shape [DESIGN SUGGESTION]
> A tinkerer package: Tool proficiencies (Thieves' Tools, Smith's Tools, Tinker's Tools) plus a thrown-weapon trick.
> This line is the author's design suggestion, not text from any book.

## Skill by skill

Wiki text is quoted from the Fear & Hunger wiki's skill lists (https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H2 and https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H1). Fit: 🟢 Close, 🟡 Partial, ⚪ Loose, none = no SRD comparable found. Fit notes are the author's judgment; the passages themselves are in [[5e - SRD passages]].

> [!hexen]- Wrench toss (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Abella)
>
> **Wiki description:** Throw your pipe wrench at the enemy with devastating force. A chance of stun. Requires pipe wrench to be equipped first.
>
> **Effect:** Throws a Pipe wrench to deal 291-437 (Attack * 6 - 20 (20% variance)) Blunt damage with a 95% Chance to inflict Concussion status to one target. Damage affected by Rev. Pipe wrench is unequipped after use.
>
> **Cost:** Free
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Stunned (Condition)|Stunned (Condition)]] (rules glossary, SRD p. 189). A status that can follow a hit; the wiki's Concussion status is not described in the SRD.

> [!hexen]- Short circuit (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Abella)
>
> **Wiki description:** Short circuit an electric lock to open locked doors. Relatively simple in practice, but short circuiting the wrong conductors might lock the door for good.
>
> **Effect:** Allows you to open doors with electronic panels in Tunnels.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Knock|Knock]] (spell, SRD p. 143). Unlocks a door or lock from range.
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Thieves' Tools|Thieves' Tools]] (tool, SRD p. 94). Tool for picking locks and disarming traps (DC 15).

> [!hexen]- Trapcraft (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Abella)
>
> **Wiki description:** Craft bear traps and booby traps from different utility items. Used in the Crafting menu.
>
> **Effect:** Unlocks recipes for: - Bear trap - Booby trap
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Tinker's Tools|Tinker's Tools]] (tool, SRD p. 94). Lists Hunting Trap among the items it crafts.

> [!hexen]- Weaponcraft (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Abella)
>
> **Wiki description:** Use your imagination to craft unique weapons from different utility items. Used in the Crafting menu.
>
> **Effect:** Unlocks recipes for: - Sandman's kiss - Reaper's rake - Lantern flail - Meat grinder
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Smith's Tools|Smith's Tools]] (tool, SRD p. 93). Crafts melee weapons and armor.
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Tinker's Tools|Tinker's Tools]] (tool, SRD p. 94). Lists Musket and Pistol among its crafts.
