# 5e - Soul - Domination

**Contestants in the wiki's tables:** D'arce

> [!tip] Suggested 5e shape [DESIGN SUGGESTION]
> A duelist package: the Dodge action, an Opportunity-Attack style counter, speed from Haste, and shoving enemies Prone.
> This line is the author's design suggestion, not text from any book.

## Skill by skill

Wiki text is quoted from the Fear & Hunger wiki's skill lists (https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H2 and https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H1). Fit: 🟢 Close, 🟡 Partial, ⚪ Loose, none = no SRD comparable found. Fit notes are the author's judgment; the passages themselves are in [[5e - SRD passages]].

> [!hexen]- Defence stance (F&H1 · Soul skills · D'arce)
>
> **Wiki description:** A special combat stance to prevent severe physical trauma. A skill taught to all the squires at the Rondon military.
>
> **Effect:** Applies Perfect Guard and Evasion Buff statuses.
>
> **Cost:** Free
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Dodge (Action)|Dodge (Action)]] (rules glossary, SRD p. 181). Attacks against you have Disadvantage and you make Dexterity saves with Advantage until your next turn.

> [!hexen]- Fast attack (F&H1 · Soul skills · D'arce)
>
> **Wiki description:** With swift movements - you gain an additional turn.
>
> **Effect:** Applies Speed Buff status.
>
> **Cost:** Free
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Haste|Haste]] (spell, SRD p. 139). Doubles Speed and grants an extra action for the duration.

> [!hexen]- Counter (F&H1 · Soul skills · D'arce)
>
> **Wiki description:** Stay alert for enemy attacks to parry them the instant they land.
>
> **Effect:** Applies Counter status.
>
> **Cost:** Free
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Opportunity Attacks|Opportunity Attacks]] (rules glossary, SRD p. 185). A Reaction attack triggered by a creature leaving your reach.
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Deflect Attacks (Monk)|Deflect Attacks (Monk)]] (class feature, SRD p. 51). A Reaction that reduces an incoming hit's damage.

> [!hexen]- Leg sweep (F&H1 · Soul skills · D'arce)
>
> **Wiki description:** A high finesse arcing attack that can only be performed with light bladed weapons.
>
> **Effect:** Destroys the legs of an opponent. / (Also breaks doors in a single hit)
>
> **Cost:** 20 Mind
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Unarmed Strike|Unarmed Strike]] (rules glossary, SRD p. 190). The Shove option can cause the Prone condition.
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Prone (Condition)|Prone (Condition)]] (rules glossary, SRD p. 186). What the Prone condition does to a creature.
