# 5e - Soul - Chaotic

**Contestants in the wiki's tables:** Pav

> [!tip] Suggested 5e shape [DESIGN SUGGESTION]
> A brawler's-initiative package: Alert-style initiative plus a Haste-like burst of extra actions for allies.
> This line is the author's design suggestion, not text from any book.

## Skill by skill

Wiki text is quoted from the Fear & Hunger wiki's skill lists (https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H2 and https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H1). Fit: 🟢 Close, 🟡 Partial, ⚪ Loose, none = no SRD comparable found. Fit notes are the author's judgment; the passages themselves are in [[5e - SRD passages]].

> [!hexen]- Bury the trauma (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Pav)
>
> **Wiki description:** Bury your trauma and fears. This reduces your sanity decline while exploring. You can deal with the psychiatric collapse and the combat fatigue once you're in a safer place. After all this...
>
> **Effect:** Heals 1 Body every 20 seconds in the overworld at night or non-safe indoor areas. ⚠ *(known bug listed on the wiki)*
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Cure Wounds|Cure Wounds]] (spell, SRD p. 121). Restores Hit Points, though not gradually over time.

> [!hexen]- En garde (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Pav)
>
> **Wiki description:** Long combat experience has prepared you for fights. Prepare for an attack to gain advantage by pressing [E].
>
> **Effect:** Press [E] on the overworld right before engaging with an enemy to get a free attack.
>
> **Cost:** Free
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Alert (feat)|Alert (feat)]] (feat, SRD p. 87). Improves Initiative so you act earlier in combat.

> [!hexen]- Order, Charge! (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Pav)
>
> **Wiki description:** Give orders to your team to charge and attack the enemy without wasting their turns.
>
> **Effect:** Every single party member will automatically attack again at the end of the turn including the main character.
>
> **Cost:** 40 Mind
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Haste|Haste]] (spell, SRD p. 139). Gives the target an additional action that can include the Attack action (one attack).
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Action Surge (Fighter)|Action Surge (Fighter)]] (class feature, SRD p. 48). Gives one additional action once per rest.

> [!hexen]- Order, Defend! (F&H2 · Unused skills · Pav)
>
> **Unlock:** Pav (Chaotic soul)
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - none: no SRD 5.2.1 passage found that covers this skill.
