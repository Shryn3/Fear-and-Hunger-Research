# 5e - Soul - Solitary

**Contestants in the wiki's tables:** Levi

> [!tip] Suggested 5e shape [DESIGN SUGGESTION]
> A marksman package: ranged-attack bonuses in the manner of the Archery feat.
> This line is the author's design suggestion, not text from any book.

## Skill by skill

Wiki text is quoted from the Fear & Hunger wiki's skill lists (https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H2 and https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H1). Fit: 🟢 Close, 🟡 Partial, ⚪ Loose, none = no SRD comparable found. Fit notes are the author's judgment; the passages themselves are in [[5e - SRD passages]].

> [!hexen]- Gun proficiency (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Levi)
>
> **Wiki description:** Proficiency with firearms gives you greater accuracy while shooting. A passive skill.
>
> **Effect:** Gives +30% Hit Rate when Lugr Pistol, Rifle .303 Mk I or 12-gauge Trenchgun are equipped in battle.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟡 **Partial** — [[5e - SRD passages#Pistol and Musket (weapon table)|Pistol and Musket (weapon table)]] (weapon table, SRD p. 91). The SRD weapon table lists a Pistol and a Musket as Martial Ranged weapons; proficiency is a flat rule, not a percentage hit rate.
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Archery (feat)|Archery (feat)]] (feat, SRD p. 87). A +2 bonus to attack rolls with Ranged weapons.

> [!hexen]- Gunslinger (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Levi)
>
> **Wiki description:** Advanced use of pistols. Precise shots can occasionally dismember opponents. Used on field.
>
> **Effect:** Allows you to dismember enemies in the overworld with Lugr Pistol.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - none: no SRD 5.2.1 passage found that covers this skill.

> [!hexen]- Executioner (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Levi)
>
> **Wiki description:** Advanced use of trenchguns. You are able to execute a stunned enemy by shooting it again on the field.
>
> **Effect:** No effect. ⚠ *(known bug listed on the wiki)*
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - none: no SRD 5.2.1 passage found that covers this skill.

> [!hexen]- Marksmanship (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Levi)
>
> **Wiki description:** Advanced use of rifles. You are able to one-shot enemies with the rifle every now and then. Used on field.
>
> **Effect:** Gives a 25% chance to instantly kill most enemies in the overworld with Rifle .303 Mk I or Bayonet.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Archery (feat)|Archery (feat)]] (feat, SRD p. 87). A flat bonus to ranged attacks; the SRD has no instant-kill rule.
