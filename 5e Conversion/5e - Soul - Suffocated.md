# 5e - Soul - Suffocated

**Contestants in the wiki's tables:** Henryk

> [!tip] Suggested 5e shape [DESIGN SUGGESTION]
> A provisioner package: Cook's Utensils and endurance-style features.
> This line is the author's design suggestion, not text from any book.

## Skill by skill

Wiki text is quoted from the Fear & Hunger wiki's skill lists (https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H2 and https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H1). Fit: 🟢 Close, 🟡 Partial, ⚪ Loose, none = no SRD comparable found. Fit notes are the author's judgment; the passages themselves are in [[5e - SRD passages]].

> [!hexen]- Slow metabolism (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Henryk)
>
> **Wiki description:** Slowing down your breath and concentrating on the matters at hand. You don't need to eat quite as often. You only need few good meals a day.
>
> **Effect:** Adds +37 seconds to the Hunger timer.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - none: no SRD 5.2.1 passage found that covers this skill.

> [!hexen]- Melee proficiency (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Henryk)
>
> **Wiki description:** Proficiency with melee weapons gives you greater accuracy when fighting with a weapon you can swing. A passive skill.
>
> **Effect:** Gives +30% Hit Rate when melee weapons are equipped in battle.
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - ⚪ **Loose** — [[5e - SRD passages#Great Weapon Fighting (feat)|Great Weapon Fighting (feat)]] (feat, SRD p. 88). A melee-only improvement, to damage dice rather than hit rate.

> [!hexen]- Masterchef (F&H2 · Contestant skills · Henryk)
>
> **Wiki description:** Greater culinary skills enable you to cook meals that have special nutritional properties. These include various stat boosts.
>
> **Effect:** Unlocks recipes for: - Meatpie+ - Vegetable pie+ - Mushroom stew+ - Goulash+
>
> **Cost:** Passive
>
> **5e comparables [DESIGN NOTE]**
> - 🟢 **Close** — [[5e - SRD passages#Cook's Utensils|Cook's Utensils]] (tool, SRD p. 93). Improve food's flavor or detect spoiled food; crafts Rations.
