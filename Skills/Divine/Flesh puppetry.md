# Flesh puppetry

**Granted by:** [[God of Fear and Hunger]] (god-affinity skill, F&H2)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Description** | Puppeteer severed limbs at your disposal to attack the enemy. You need sawed off limbs to do this. |
| **Effect** | Summons hands that deals up to 1.5x of your Attack at end of the turn to a random target, the amount of attacks and the damage is based off of the amount of [arms](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Arm_(F%26H2)) (Maxes out at 4 arms) you have in your inventory. |
| **Cost** | 10 Mind, cost is applied every turn |
| **Success rate** | 95% (Physical Attack) |
| **Unlock requirement** | 2 God of Fear and Hunger affinity |

Wiki page: <https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Flesh_puppetry>
