# Brain flower

**Granted by:** [[Sylvian]] (god-affinity skill, F&H2)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Description** | Plant your personal seeds of brain flower to a fertile ground. Freshly deceased corpses being the most fertile of grounds. A brain flower replenishes sanity and mind. |
| **Effect** | Gives an option to plant Brain flower seeds on defeated enemies in the overworld. After 2:21 or 2:29 you can collect [Brain flower stigma](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Brain_flower_stigma). |
| **Cost** | Passive |
| **Unlock requirement** | 2 Sylvian affinity / 2 Vinushka affinity |
