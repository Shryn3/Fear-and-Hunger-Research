# Blood sacrifice

**Soul:** [[Radiating soul]]

**Learned by (per wiki list):** [[Samarie]]

| | |
|---|---|
| **Description** | Sacrifice your own blood for Gro-goroth, the god of destruction. A blood sacrifice will enhance your affinity with the said god. Sacrifices like these were everyday occurences back in the day. |
| **Effect** | Increases Gro-goroth affinity when used on Gro-goroth ritual circles, can be used multiple times (Except at the Abandoned House circle). ⚠ *(known bug listed on the wiki)* Removes 150 Body on use and leaves player with 1 Body, unless attempted with less than 50 Body which results in death. |
| **Cost** | Passive |
