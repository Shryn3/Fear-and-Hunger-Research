# Undergrowth awareness

**Soul:** [[Shadowed soul]]

**Learned by (per wiki list):** [[Olivia]]

| | |
|---|---|
| **Description** | You can harvest irritable and poisonous plants safely and also tell apart the plants that have potential use in your situation. A passive skill useful on the field. |
| **Effect** | Gives an option to harvest [Dark blue root](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Dark_blue_root), [Poison hemlock](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Poison_hemlock), [Nettle](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Nettle) and [Wild dagga](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Wild_dagga) in the overworld. |
| **Cost** | Passive |
