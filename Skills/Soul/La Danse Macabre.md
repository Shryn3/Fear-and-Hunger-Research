# La Danse Macabre

**Soul:** [[Enlightened soul]]

**Learned by (per wiki list):** [[O'saa]]

| | |
|---|---|
| **Description** | A ritual dance used to channel otherwordly powers to be used by the performer. Raises otherwordly powers gradually over the course of a fight. A passive skill. |
| **Effect** | Increases M.Attack at the end of each turn for 3 turns: - Turn 1 - ×150% M.Attack - Turn 2 - ×270% M.Attack - Turn 3 - ×500% M.Attack |
| **Cost** | Passive |
