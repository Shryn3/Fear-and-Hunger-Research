# Advanced botanism

**Soul:** [[Shadowed soul]]

**Learned by (per wiki list):** [[Olivia]]

| | |
|---|---|
| **Description** | The skill to condense the effective properties of herbs and plants to maximize their potential. Teaches you the recipes for condensed blue and green herbs as well as lavender. |
| **Effect** | Unlocks recipes for: - [Mixed herbs (dark blue, red)](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Mixed_herbs_(dark_blue,_red)) - [Mixed herbs (dark blue, blue)](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Mixed_herbs_(dark_blue,_blue)) - [Condensed blue](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Condensed_blue) - [Condensed green](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Condensed_green) - [Condensed lavender](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Condensed_lavender) |
| **Cost** | Passive |
