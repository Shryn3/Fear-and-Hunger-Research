# Unnamed wolf deity

**Classification (per wiki):** Unspecified classification

> [!info] No standalone wiki page was fetched for this entity; everything below is from the *The Gods* page.

First mentioned in Cahara's Forgotten Memories, in the northern regions of the world, a wolflike deity has been spotted and spoken about in lore. Very little is known about this entity, but it is rumored that it grants knowledge and skill in exchange for a steep price: the life of a loved one. The wolf deity may be one of the many forms taken by Gro-goroth, who is often described as wearing the skin of both man and beast to conceal his true nature. The fact that some followers of the Old God don a wolf mask further adds to this possibility, which has yet to be confirmed.
