# God of the Depths

**Classification (per wiki):** Old God

## Overview on *The Gods*

The **God of the Depths** is an obscure god worshipped by outcasts, the disenfranchised, and insects. It also possesses a corruptive darkness that can create mutants. A dormant trace is present in the Altar of Darkness inside the mines beneath the Dungeons. Within it lies The Gauntlet, the swallowed ruins of a temple that lead to the Heart of Darkness. The trace was killed and used to give birth to the  [[God of Fear and Hunger]]; however, it's revealed that the God of the Depths has [influence](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Beekeeper) in the present day. Their runic symbol is that of a backwards R.

*Source: [[Wiki - The Gods]]*

## Detailed page

**The God of the Depths** is an [[The Gods|Old God]] and an important deity in *[[Fear & Hunger]]*. They were a gargantuan creature with organs spread everywhere across the lower levels of the dungeons of fear and hunger. Their body also serves as a location - entering the god's mouth leads to [The Gauntlet](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Gauntlet), an end-game area.

*Source: [[Wiki - God of the Depths]]*

### Lore

The God of the Depths is one of the old deities worshiped by man, before man decided that they had enough of the old world order and wanted to supplant the Old Gods. The God of the Depths is not as well-known compared to the other Old Gods such as [[Gro-goroth]] or [[Sylvian]].  They are implied to be responsible for the sanity-draining darkness and changing layout of the [Dungeons of Fear and Hunger](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Dungeons_of_Fear_and_Hunger).[^1]

From bits of scattered information and talking to their followers in the game, it seems that the god encompasses the idea of revulsion and inescapable darkness. According to [[Ragnvaldr]], the God of the Depths is "worshiped by those who are forsaken and forgotten."[^1] The God of the Depths is closely associated with bugs and vermin; many of their followers are insectoid, such as the [scarabs](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Scarab), cockroaches, and the [Cockroach King](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Cockroach_King). Devotees of this god often seem devoid of self-awareness or higher intelligence, only capable of carrying out simple orders. Besides insects and other small creatures, those that have been exiled or trapped in darkness often turn to the God of the Depths. The [Cavedwellers](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Cavedweller), the [Body Snatcher](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Body_Snatcher), the [Butterfly](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Butterfly), and the [Mumbler](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Mumbler) are examples of mindless individuals who worship this God.

There is evidence that the God of the Depths had human worshippers as well. At a certain point in history, a mysterious sect known as the **Hermit Mages of the South** held the God of the Depths in high esteem. These reclusive practitioners were known for their rare ability to imbue [rings](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Leechmonger_ring) with life-draining magic, though little else is known about their practices beyond their name. Moreover, ancient folklore speaks of the **[Maiden of the Depths](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Maiden_of_the_Depths)**, a human woman who was supposedly chosen to be the bride of the God of the Depths.

The skill *[Mastery over Insects](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Mastery_over_Insects)* is affiliated with the god and allows one to communicate with such creatures and issue commands. In *Fear  & Hunger*, most creatures that can be conversed with using this skill act highly submissive towards the player and express love towards them as their "master." Unlike other gods, affiliation with the God of the depths is gained through fulfilling the Cockroach King's quests, as opposed to praying or making a sacrifice. The few other spells they can provide to a believer are mild offensive spells that range in usefulness and make use of insects and birds as minions.

The deity is affiliated with the [Cube of the Depths](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Cube_of_the_Depths), an essential object needed to reach [Ma'habre](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Ma'habre).

Unlike other Old Gods, the God of the Depths is present in a physical form in the dungeon. The player can enter their mouth after destroying all three of the god's hearts in the game. The god will open their mouth, and the player may enter **[The Gauntlet](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Gauntlet)**, the most difficult area in the game, filled with monsters and lethal traps.

The God of the Depths' hearts can be found in the following three places:
- On Level 7 of the dungeon, in the same area where [Le'garde](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Le'garde) can be found.
- At the bottom of the Tree dungeon in the [Thicket](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Thicket).
- At the end of the present-day Golden Temple, where the aged [[Francóis]] is found.

In *[[Fear & Hunger 2 Termina|Fear & Hunger 2: Termina]]*, it is revealed that the god died in 1590 after one of the four heroes killed their three hearts in *Fear & Hunger* and that their body was used as a vessel to give birth to the [[God of Fear and Hunger]]. However, as an older god cannot truly die, the God of the Depths' influence persists, and the [Beekeepers](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Beekeeper) stand as a remnant of the God of the Depths' enduring influence in modern times.[^2]

> [!warning] The wiki marks the following as speculation.

- In *[[Fear & Hunger 2 Termina|Fear & Hunger 2: Termina]]*, if you bring [[O'saa]] to the basement of the [Mayor's Manor](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Old_Town_-_Mayor's_Manor), he will comment on the nature of the strange sacs found inside, calling them "manifestations of the black."  This could possibly be another case of Depths' influencing things in modern day.

*Source: [[Wiki - God of the Depths]]*

### Trivia

- In *Fear & Hunger*, there are totems and statues scattered among the dungeon, where the player character can pray for an old god in order to increase affinity with said deity and unlock their spells on the Hexen Table. While there is a multitude of these statues where one can pray to [[Alll-mer]], [[Gro-goroth]], and [[Sylvian]], the only place where the character may pray to the God of the Depths is at the Gro-gorth statue in [Center Square](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Center_Square) in Old [Ma'habre](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Ma'habre), which might be an indication that the god's cult has dwindled greatly over the years.
- In older versions of *Fear & Hunger*, the player could also sacrifice party members for the God of the Depths. The idea was that the act would alter the layout of the dungeon later in the game, as the deity is linked to the dungeons heavily, but the feature was eventually abandoned before being implemented.
- The God of the Depths is referred to in-game by both male and female pronouns on different occasions, possibly due to different interpretations of the deity's mythology or as a result of  [Miro Haverinen](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Miro_Haverinen) speaking English as a second language. For this reason, this article was written using gender neutral terms.
- The God of the Depths' design is seemingly inspired by the underwater shipwreck of the *USS Saratoga*, specifically the Hawse Hole at the bow of the destroyed ship.

*Source: [[Wiki - God of the Depths]]*
