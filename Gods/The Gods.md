## Trivia (from the wiki)

- According to game creator [Miro Haverinen](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Miro_Haverinen), it is natural for diverse cultures in the *Fear & Hunger* universe to worship distinct deities and have their own Gods. He also suggests that these Gods may either be entirely different entities or interpretations of the same Gods in varying ways.
- Miro has also stated that he has been trying to introduce the notion that all the lore concerning Gods in the games is human-created information, and as such, the accuracy of this information remains uncertain. Questions regarding the extent of Gods' interaction with living beings or what qualifies as a "God" are intentionally left open-ended.
- There are three unofficially named gods whose names are present in the game files on the clock in the [Museum](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Museum), but not mentioned in-game. Their names are Amon for the first sigil, Turkil for the seventh, and Sarko for the tenth, respectively. The name Amon shares the same name as the City of the Sun in [Abyssonia](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Abyssonia)
- The first sigil in the museum clock also appears in the [Book of enlightenment](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Book_of_enlightenment) and in the [[Heartless One]]'s Curse of Light spell. The tenth sigil is used by the [Black witch](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Black_witch) to teleport around in Ma'habre and appears in the [Ancient book](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Ancient_book) and in the Level 2 [Basement](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Basement) ritual.
- Miro revealed the name of the white-robed new god we see on the hall to be "Sotra Ulft" [^1]
