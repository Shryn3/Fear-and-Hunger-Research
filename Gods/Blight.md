# Blight

**Classification (per wiki):** Blight

## Detailed page

**Blights** is an [enemy](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Enemies_List_F%26H1) encountered in *[[Fear & Hunger]]*.

*Source: [[Wiki - Blight]]*

### Lore

> ***"Before men, the lizardmen roamed as the dominating lifeform. Like us, they too desired to ascend higher. What remains of those ancient times are the forms."***
>
> *- The New Gods when asked about the Blight.*

As per the teachings of the [[The Gods|New Gods]] within [Ma'habre](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Ma'habre), the Blights are believed to be [Lizardmen](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Lizardman) who have chosen to ascend to godhood within the realm of [the Void](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Void). They represent New Gods in their own right.

An ancient stone slab unearthed from an archaeological site in northern [Rondon](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Rondon), and exhibited in modern times at the [Bohemia National Museum](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Museum) in [Prehevil](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Prehevil), showcases a depiction of "The Nine Chromatic Blights." This imagery suggests that humans have conducted studies and delved into the nature of these deities, who were also explored in "The Book of Homer".

*Source: [[Wiki - Blight]]*

### Trivia

- If the player stands still while the blight flies above them, it won't initiate the combat and instead will simply fly over the player.
- Using [Demon seed](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Demon_seed) on a blight is the only way to have the [kid Demon](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Kid_Demon) present in [The Void](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Void), as they will not follow you in.
- Giving the blight a [Yellow vial](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Yellow_vial) to end the battle peacefully kills it instead of aborting the battle, causing it to also be dead in the overworld.  ⚠ *(known bug listed on the wiki)*

*Source: [[Wiki - Blight]]*
