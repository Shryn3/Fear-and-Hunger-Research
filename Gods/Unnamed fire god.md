# Unnamed fire god

**Classification (per wiki):** Unspecified classification

> [!info] No standalone wiki page was fetched for this entity; everything below is from the *The Gods* page.

In *Fear & Hunger*, Nas'hrah briefly mentions an unnamed fire god if one attempts a Marriage of Flesh with him. In this scenario, the proposer is killed by being burned to death, with the act labeled as a "sacrifice for the fire god himself." Although it's also most likely that Nas'hrah is referring to himself, notably, both Gro-goroth and Vinushka in the series are linked to fire skills, also raising the possibility that either of these two Old Gods could be the fire god alluded to by Nas'hrah rather than a third one.

*Source: [[Wiki - The Gods]]*
