# Gro-goroth

**Classification (per wiki):** Old God

## Overview on *The Gods*

**Gro-goroth**, **the destroyer of men**,[^1] is the God of destruction and human sacrifice. He wishes for blood to be spilled in his name. Gro-goroth is a curious God, more so than many of the other Old Gods, and wore the skin of men and women to relieve the feeling of fear.[^2] Some believe he still walks among men, masked under the skin of people and hanging bodies. His gift to humans is blood magic, although its potency wanes with his presence. His runic symbol depicts an eclipse.

*Source: [[Wiki - The Gods]]*

## Detailed page

**Gro-goroth** is the [[The Gods|Old God]] of destruction and human sacrifices. In *[[Fear & Hunger]]*, his **traces** are encountered as the final [boss](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Enemies_List_F%26H1) of [Ending B](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Endings_F%26H1).

*Source: [[Wiki - Gro-goroth]]*

### Lore

Gro-goroth is an Old God who originated from the green hue. Being the god of destruction and human sacrifices, he wishes for blood to be spilled in his name. He embodies death, chaos, and war. In the world of *Fear & Hunger*, destruction is seen as a part of the natural order, because death is necessary to pave the way for nascence and growth.[^3] Gro-goroth gifted **blood magic** to mankind, and almost all spells that are associated with him (such as *Hurting* and *Black Orb*) are used offensively. Gro-goroth is stated to be a curious god, a unique trait amongst his kind. He is said to wear the bodies and skin of men and women alike in order to "relieve" the feeling of fear when interacting with humans.[^4] Gro-goroth's sigil depicts an eclipse, as seen on [eclipse talismans](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Items_List_F%26H1#accessories-0) and the tails side of coin flips.

One can strengthen their affinity with Gro-goroth in a variety of ways. In *Fear & Hunger,* the player can do this by sacrificing teammates at ritual circles, praying to Gro-goroth at a ritual circle, praying to his statue in [Ma'habre](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Ma'habre), or by partaking in the [Wolfmasks' cannibal ritual](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Wolfmasks). In *[[Fear & Hunger 2 Termina|Fear & Hunger 2: Termina]]*, the player can raise their affinity by drawing Gro-goroth's sigil at an [[Ritual Circles F&H2|asymmetric circle]],  sacrificing teammates at Gro-goroth circles, or by performing a [blood sacrifice](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Blood_sacrifice_(F%26H2)) at a Gro-goroth circle. The act of [engraving](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Engrave) Gro-goroth's sigil on the face raises one's attack power. Gro-goroth grants his followers incredible power but also corrupts them—the [Black Witch](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Black_Witch) and [Yellow Mages](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Yellow_mage) are able to harness Gro-goroth's power in battle with deadly effect, but in turn are very fragile in body. The Black Witch in particular is described by the New Gods as a mere "slave to Gro-goroth."[^5]

Like most of his kin, Gro-goroth has long since started to fade from the world, disgusted with the selfishness, narrow-mindedness, and ambition of humans who were incapable of comprehending the purity and beauty of the ideas he represents. As he fades away, so does his gift of blood magic to mankind.[^6] However, there are those who believe Gro-goroth still roams the mortal realm, hidden beneath the flesh of human beings and the macabre display of hanging bodies.[^4]

In *Fear & Hunger 2: Termina*,  [it is said that](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skin_Bible_-_Vinushka) he and his antithesis [[Sylvian]] are revealed to have mated together, producing [[Vinushka]], the God of Nature.

*Source: [[Wiki - Gro-goroth]]*

### Trivia

- According to [Miro Haverinen](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Miro_Haverinen), Gro-goroth's name was invented "on the spot" while hosting a tabletop role-playing game session for his schoolmates, and carries no greater meaning.[^7]
- If the player has [[Nas'hrah]] in their party while beginning the fight, Nas'hrah will spurt a short dialogue about destroying the Old God, prompting the Traces of Gro-goroth to instantly obliterate Nas'hrah (this means the player must face the battle one party member short). However, this cutscene happens regardless of whether Nas'hrah is in the party, because the game does not actually check if the player has Nash'rah with them.  ⚠ *(known bug listed on the wiki)*

### Additional Tormentpedia statements not found in this note
*From [[Tormentpedia - Traces of Gro-goroth]]. Some may restate the main wiki in different words; real-world comparison trivia is not reproduced.*

- This being is what is left of what was once Gro-Goroth, the Old God of human sacrifices.
- The player gains alignment with him by sacrificing teammates in ritual circles or taking part in the Wolfmasks cult, where its participants maim each other and feast upon their own flesh in honor of Gro-goroth.
- He grants his followers and disciples incredible power but also corrupts them - the Black Witch and Yellow Mages are incredibly powerful beings in the realm of magic, but physically fragile.
- Since humankind took over Ma'habre, the Old God, like most of his kindred, began to distance himself, disgusted by the selfishness, narrow-mindedness, and ambition of mankind as they have no understanding of the purity and beauty of the concepts he represents, as each human interprets the pure ideas of the Gods differently.
- Following this departure, Gro-goroth relocated to the darkest depths of the Dungeons of Fear and Hunger, past the Altar of Darkness, within the body of the God of the Depths.
- His symbol is the intersecting of two rings, seen on the tails part of the coin flip.
