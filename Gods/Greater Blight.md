# Greater Blight



**Classification (per wiki):** Blight



## Detailed page

**Greater Blight** is a unique [enemy](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Enemies_List_F%26H1) encountered in *[[Fear & Hunger]]*.

*Source: [[Wiki - Greater Blight]]*

### Lore

> ***"Before men, the lizardmen roamed as the dominating lifeform. Like us, they too desired to ascend higher. What remains of those ancient times are the forms. Greater blight is something that rivals the powers of the new gods even in this day and age. It is truly curious how powerful those ancient relics were in their true prime."***  
> 
> *- The New Gods when asked about the Greater blight.*

Greater blights are beings of [Lizardmen](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Lizardman) origin who have transcended the confines of [the Void](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Void). Remarkably, they continue to wield powers that can stand toe-to-toe with contemporary [[The Gods|New Gods]]; it is even hinted that during their prime, their might may have eclipsed even their current impressive power.

*Source: [[Wiki - Greater Blight]]*
