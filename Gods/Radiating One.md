# Radiating One

**Classification (per wiki):** New God

## Overview on *The Gods*

The **Radiating One** is a recently-introduced New God featured in *Fear & Hunger 2: Termina*. Little information is currently available on this particular deity. Those who summon him at an Imperfect Circle may interact with him as a kind of merchant, offering valuable items in exchange for rust-colored pearls.

*Source: [[Wiki - The Gods]]*

## Detailed page

The **Radiating One** is a non-playable character and [vendor](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Vendors) encountered in *[[Fear & Hunger 2 Termina|Fear & Hunger 2: Termina]]*. This deity is considered to be a [[The Gods|New God]].

*Source: [[Wiki - Radiating One]]*

### Lore

Limited information exists about the Radiating One, except that he is classified as a New God. Curiously, the item used to summon him on Ritual Circles is an antique portrait photograph from the late 1800s featuring an unidentified young man. Strangely, the person's face in the photograph has been deliberately scratched over and is now unrecognizable.

*Source: [[Wiki - Radiating One]]*

### Encounter

The Radiating One can be summoned at an Imperfect [[Ritual Circles F&H2|Ritual Circle]] by sacrificing a [Portrait of a young man](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Portrait_of_a_young_man).

Several valuable items are sold using [Rust-coloured pearls](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Rust-coloured_pearl):

- 8 [Leechmonger ring](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Leechmonger_ring)
- 4 [Ring of wraiths](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Ring_of_wraiths_(F%26H2))
- 9 [Yggaegetsu amulet](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Yggaegetsu_amulet)
- 4 [12-gauge Trenchgun](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/12-gauge_Trenchgun)
- 10 [Mauler](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Mauler)

If the player does not have any rust-coloured pearls within their inventory whilst summoning the Radiating One, the player will get a snarky comment from the Radiating One for wasting his time.

*Source: [[Wiki - Radiating One]]*

### Trivia

- While the Radiating One is portrayed as having the Radiating soul within the storyline, in terms of gameplay mechanics, players cannot collect it in *Fear & Hunger 2: Termina*. They must obtain it from [[Samarie]] within the game.
- The Radiating One's design is a reference to [Kyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyo_(musician)) from the band [Dir En Grey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dir_En_Grey), as confirmed by the creator on [twitter](https://twitter.com/Conor_Isonfire/status/1687811754741350401?s=20).

*Source: [[Wiki - Radiating One]]*
