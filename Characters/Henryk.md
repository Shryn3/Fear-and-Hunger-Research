## Biography

> ***"Even in this hellish situation, I end up cooking. And the craziest thing is that I haven't been this inspired, like ever. I'm seeing new combinations in my head, my palette is expanding thanks to all the new local ingredients... Making food like it's the last day of my life."***  
> 
> *- Henryk, at [Restaurant Bílý Vůl](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Restaurant_B%C3%ADl%C3%BD_V%C5%AFl).*

**Henryk** is a Non-playable [Side Character](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Characters_List_F%26H2#%23Side_Characters-0) and [Potential Enemy](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Enemies_List_F%26H2#Contestants-0) in *[[Fear & Hunger 2 Termina|Fear & Hunger 2: Termina]]*.

He is a former artist and talented chef, and his skills include cooking exceptionally filling meals, proficiency in using cutting tools and the ability to make improvised cooking stations. Henryk is one of the fourteen contestants arriving in [Prehevil](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Prehevil) that were forced to participate in the [Festival of Termina](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Termina).

*Source: [[Wiki - Henryk]]*

### Lore

A chef forced to be a contestant in the [Festival of Termina](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Termina).

Henryk is a 32-year-old chef from the Kingdom of [Rondon](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Rondon) with an acute taste for ingredients. Not much is known about his past, but he will partially open up about how he became a chef to the player after moving to a kitchen. During his younger years, he worked at a tavern called Klimkov's which was owned by his parents, where he would help around the kitchen by cooking meals. He later became a bartender for some time, where he had a fling with a female pianist. Henryk had always dreamed becoming someone important by creating something great, whether it be a musician, artist or actor - he eventually "dipped his toes in everything", but did not take well to rejection and in the end gave up those plans.

Eventually, Henryk ran out of money, so out of necessity, he decided to return his parents' tavern where he started to cook meals again. He eventually realised that he had a great natural talent for the culinary arts, and in the end his skills flourished to the point where Klimkov's became a famous place to eat, even becoming the subject of an article in a newspaper. Now embracing this talent, he frequently travelled to find new culinary inspiration - eventually landing him in the city of [Prehevil](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Prehevil), as he seemed fascinated by local delicacies and ingredients.[^1]

*Source: [[Wiki - Henryk]]*

### Personality

Henryk is very passionate about cooking food, and it is the main thing he seems to enjoy in life. He can be quite laid back but has low self-esteem, often trying to seek the approval of other contestants with his cooking and will seem devastated if anyone doesn't like it. He can also be impulsive sometimes, making perverted remarks towards [[Abella]] at the train and starting a fight with [[Caligura]] that potentially gets him killed. 

As the festival progresses and the deadline approaches, he becomes increasingly anxious about the time limit mentioned by Per'kele, with the thoughts of the third day starting to consume his thoughts. He may resort to extreme measures if the opportunity presents itself...

### Trivia

- Henryk is a Polish male given name of Germanic origin. It means 'ruler of the home' or 'lord of the house'. The name seems appropriate for his cooking profession.
- Henryk's dialogue changes depending on which protagonist was chosen: if you play as [[Abella]] and talk to him at the Mayor's Manor, his dialogues will be flirtatious. If you play as [[Marina]] or [[Levi]], he will call the player character a kid.
- If you are not playing Abella and recruit her first, Henryk will disappear on Day 1 Morning and can only be found Day 1 Evening as the [Gentleman](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Gentleman). Alternatively, if you help Henryk at the manor, Abella will disappear and mutate into [Chaugnar](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Chaugnar).
- Henryk and [[Daan]] are currently the only contestants whose moonscorched forms are lucid enough to offer goods to the player without defeating them in battle. The Gentleman will give the player character a key if he deems their dinner conversation acceptable enough and the [Pocketcat](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Pocketcat) can exchange wares for decapitated contestant heads.
  - Interestingly, both moonscorched forms share the similarity of preferring 'gentlemantly' behaviors. This is likely to be coincidental considering the lack of deep connections between the two, however.
- The tavern his parents owned is called Klimkov's. That would suggest that his surname is Klimkov.[^2]
- During the beta version of the game, he used to mention his zodiac sign was Sagittarius.
- Henyrk tells a Monty Python reference ("'tis but a flesh wound") if one of his arms is cut during a battle.
- Game creator [Miro Haverinen](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Miro_Haverinen) expressed interest in making Henryk a playable character due to [his unremarkable nature](https://x.com/happy_paintings/status/1718547149288251509?s=20): as Henryk as one of the most everyday individuals alongside [[Tanaka]], developing diverse gameplay for him would be challenging.
