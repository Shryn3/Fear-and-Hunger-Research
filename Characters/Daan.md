## Biography

> ***"To be frank, that sigil has occupied my thoughts for the most part. I just need more answers... This quest for information is pretty much the only thing pushing me forward anymore at this point."***  
> 
> *- Daan, at the [PRHVL Bop](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/PRHVL_Bop)*

**Daan**, also known as **Daniël**, is a Playable Character and a potential [Party Member](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Characters_List_F%26H2#Party_Members-0) in *[[Fear & Hunger 2 Termina|Fear & Hunger 2: Termina]]*.

A doctor with knowledge of both modern and occult medicine, his skill set is focused on curing different status ailments and analysing possible weaknesses in enemy anatomy. Daan is one of the fourteen contestants arriving in [Prehevil](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Prehevil) that were forced to participate in the [Festival of Termina](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Termina).

*Source: [[Wiki - Daan]]*

### Lore

Daan grew up in the Kingdom of [Rondon](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Rondon), though the exact place of his birth is unknown. As a toddler, he traveled across [Europa](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Europa) alongside his parents, followers of the [[The Gods|Old God]] [[Sylvian]]. Daan's parents were members of the [Bunnymasks](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Bunnymasks), and he would often see them putting on rabbit masks while naked and heading to the meadows with others. Daan hated his childhood, for his parents prioritized their religion over caring for him. Their attempt to pass upon him the healing gift of Sylvian further emphasized their detachment from his emotional needs.

One day, his parents never came back after spending too much time in the meadows and succumbing to the ritual. Left alone at thirteen, Daan faced a choice: try to make an honest living or delve into pickpocketing. However, fate steered him towards an unexpected path—an apprenticeship under a butler. While serving Baron Eihner Von Dutch, Daan developed a taste for the finer things and a mastery of etiquette.

Over time, he became acquainted with the Baron's daughter, Elise. They would talk about his childhood and the madness brought on by his cultist parents. The Baron was interested in the cult and how Sylvian's gift of healing worked when he learned about Daan's upbringing. In exchange for his knowledge of Sylvian, Eihner, a doctor himself, would teach him about the modern ways of medical practice. Although he would never have an official doctor's diploma, Daan became able to give medical aid due to this apprenticeship.

As Daan matured into adulthood, he found love in the arms of Elise, blessed by the Baron himself. However, their romance unfolded during times of conflict between the [Eastern Union](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Eastern_Union) and the [Bremen Empire](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Bremen_Empire), drawing neighboring lands into the fray. Amidst this turmoil, Rondon enlisted all able-bodied men, Daan included, to take up arms and join the fight.

Choosing to serve as a medic or as an infantry troop, Daan bore witness to unspeakable horrors during the dark days of war. Letters from home kept his sanity intact, but soon the letters stopped appearing. After the Kingdom of Rondon withdrew from the war, he rushed back to his home only to find his wife Elise and her father Baron Eihner Von Dutch deceased in the basement. Occult practice had completely consumed the Baron and he had sacrificed Elise to conduct a ritual to a [[Sulfur God|certain god]] unknown to Daan. Out of desperation, Daan attempted to resurrect his wife by trading his left eye in a ritual. Unfortunately, his attempts to save her were unsuccessful.

Daan was heartbroken and felt complete despair as he tried to uncover how they died and what ritual was being conducted - which was when he found a couple of mentions of a town called [Prehevil](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Prehevil) in the Baron's documents. He packed up to seek the truth, unbeknownst to the fact that he was going to partake in a twisted [reunion](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Stitches) after becoming one of the contestants of the [Festival of Termina](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Termina).

*Source: [[Wiki - Daan]]*

### Personality

Daan is a smart and inquisitive man who uses his medical knowledge and experience with the occult to help make sense of the world around him. 

He is fairly independent, quickly setting off on his own to investigate Prehevil. However, he is happy to help others when the need arises, such as when he offers to mix drinks for contestants in [PRHVL Bop](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/PRHVL_Bop). Daan can be found caring for the townspeople when he is not the chosen playable character, and he is often polite and even caring towards the other contestants, indicating he has a compassionate side to him. Despite Daan's polite exterior, he also has a mischievous and condescending side. [[Karin]] takes the brunt of this, often the target of jokes or comments at her expense, but Daan also targets [[O'saa]] and the other contestants on occasion. None of his jokes seem particularly mean-spirited but rather small jabs or hints at a less friendly side to his personality. 

Daan has an interest in bartending and mixology, which can be seen during his interactions at PRHVL Bop. Based on the player's descriptions of his drinks, he seems to have a talent for it. His signature drink, according to him, is an old-fashioned.

Though Daan is outwardly friendly, he suffers from severe depression stemming from the mysterious death of his wife, Elise. It is also possible that he has been suffering from depression for a long time, considering his traumatic childhood. During various party talks, Daan makes reference to the stress he has faced due to his childhood and he is shown to be protective towards the younger contestants like [[Marina]] and [[Levi]], possibly as a way for him to help other children avoid the same situations. One example of this is Daan refusing to let Marina drink liquor because of her age.

*Source: [[Wiki - Daan]]*

### Trivia

- Game creator [Miro Haverinen](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Miro_Haverinen) has stated that Daan was born in the *Fear & Hunger* equivalent of real-life Netherlands, which has yet to be named and mentioned in the series.
- Daan has a unique transformation scene after the time expires in the festival. He will receive a visit from the [Pocketcat](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Pocketcat_(F%26H2)#PocketF%26H2%29) warning him that the inevitable is coming and starting to encourage it by embracing the Moonscorch, giving his mask and the jacket to Daan, causing the ending of the transformation process.
- Daan is said to have been followed by Pocketcat since before he arrived in Prehevil.
  - Although Pocketcat had talked to Daan before his arrival in Prehevil and Pocketcat talks about Daan's motivations in his moonscorch sequence as if remembering Daan personally, none of Pocketcat's merchant or battle dialogue toward Daan seems to differ from his dialogue towards other playable contestants.
- Daan does not have an official doctor's diploma despite being considered the doctor among the contestants, as he explicitly admits in interactions with Karin.
  - In the same conversation, he states he was an apprentice under an official doctor and is still able to give medical aid.
- [Pocketcat's room](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Golden_Gates#Pocketcat%27s_room) changes after Daan is moonscorched. The room is initially filled with children's toys and burlap sacks with a crescent moon and star hanging from the ceiling, but after he moonscorches it turns into a crime scene with the crescent moon and star both becoming only silhouettes.
  - Some party talks between the rooms differ after Daan is moonscorched. It is pointed out that the crime scene feels staged, like a movie scene.

> [!warning] The wiki marks the following as speculation.

- It is heavily implied that Daan has connections with [Stitches](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Stitches) and [Needles](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Needles), as well as their worshipped [[Sulfur God]], though he is not fully aware of it:
  - When diagnosing the two enemies' corpses, Daan will note that Stitches resembles *Elise*, his late wife, and that Needles resembles *Baron*, his late father-in-law, despite their transformation.
  - Both enemies are speculated to be associated with the Sulfur cult, judging by their appearance and colour schemes which resemble the likes of [[Per'kele]] and the [Janitor](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Janitor).
  - Daan's character history mentions Baron's apparent fascination with [Prehevil](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Prehevil), the city in which the Sulfur God is worshipped. It is implied that Baron sacrificed himself and Elise to this god.
  - When playing as Daan or having him as a party member in the battle against Needles, Needles starts to frown. This does not happen with any other contestant.
  - When playing as [[O'saa]], Daan's dialogue in the white mold apartments contains an option to tell him about the sigil on the floor. He insists that the player gives more answers. When told that the sigil belongs to the Sulfur God, Daan is disturbed by this and asks himself, *"How could've he known of this god then..."* The Sulfur sigil on the floor may be the sigil he found next to his wife and father-in-law, implied by his disturbed demeanor and insistence on asking.
- Pocketcat's room becoming a crime scene may reference Daan's insistence to find answers about his wife and father-in-law's deaths, with the crescent moon and star becoming silhouettes to indicate a muted connection to the Moon God. This can imply that Daan, even after becoming Pocketcat, harbors lingering feelings about his life as a human.
- Since Pocketcat's nature is to meddle as ordered by the Moon God, it could mean Daan is being meddled with so as to not fulfill a greater role.
  - Daan searched for answers behind the Sulfur God's influence on his life. The moon god is said to be a jealous god who interferes with other gods to prevent them from growing more powerful. As the Sulfur God is mostly obscure and obscurity causes a god's influence to dwindle, it could mean that Daan's actions to seek answers would have caused the Sulfur God to grow in influence if not interfered with by Rher.
