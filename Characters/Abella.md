## Biography

> ***"The reason why I like engineering and mechanics is that it always follows a clear logic. But I just can't figure this one out!"***  
> 
> — Abella, when examining the [train](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Train)

**Abella** is a playable character and a potential [party member](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Characters_List_F%26H2#Party_Members-0) in *[[Fear & Hunger 2 Termina|Fear & Hunger 2: Termina]]*.

She is a mechanic, and as such is adept at crafting her own makeshift weapons and traps. Along with mechanical skills, she also understands more complicated machinery. Abella is one of the fourteen contestants arriving in [Prehevil](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Prehevil) who were forced to participate in the [Festival of Termina](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Termina).

*Source: [[Wiki - Abella]]*

### Lore

Despite her homeland having suffered greatly from the First Great War, Abella had a serene and balanced childhood with her parents and many siblings. While the northern capital of [Oldegård](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Oldeg%C3%A5rd) was rebuilding its cities in the aftermath of the destruction, news of hostilities in eastern [Europa](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Europa) was broadcast on the radio. Abella, being too young to participate in the defense of her borders, had to find an alternate way to contribute to her home front. With her innate talent for mechanics and electronics, she ultimately decided to pursue these fields of study.

Abella's inherent nature, as the eldest child in her family, was to take care of those around her. However, with many of her close relatives having marched off to battle, she felt frustrated by her inability to help people more directly. Although she had already matured into adulthood, Abella's expertise in engineering made her an essential player on the disarrayed home front. With the missing men now dying on the battlefield, it was necessary for someone to step up and cover for them, and Abella knew that no one else could. She would shoulder the burden and the increasing workload, as it was the only way to ensure that the home front remained functional in the face of the ongoing war.

During one stormy night, Abella trudged through the rain-soaked alleyway on her way home from a long day of work. Suddenly, she noticed a foreign man who tried to run away but collapsed weakly after a few meters. Abella quickly realized that the man had been shot in the belly. Searching his possessions, she found documents indicating that he was from [Rondon](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Rondon), one of Oldegård's allied countries. Regardless of whether she chose to assist him or not, Abella would soon discover that the man was a member of an underground resistance group called the "[Nameless Liberty Underground](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Nameless_Liberty_Underground)" (NLU). The organization had been quietly brewing beneath the surface since the beginning of the First Great War and worked across Europa to thwart the advancements of both the [Bremen Empire](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Bremen_Empire) and the [Eastern Union](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Eastern_Union). She would eventually meet with a member of the NLU and talk briefly about the current situation in eastern Europa.

Abella's engineering expertise was greatly valued by the Nameless Liberty Underground, which was struggling to maintain membership. They requested that she join them at their headquarters. After some time, the NLU had gathered enough information to unravel parts of a large scheme. The Eastern Union was working on something ill-boding in the country of Bohemia, Prehevil, and the Bremens wanted to get their hands on the project by any means possible. When the Bremen army finally occupied [Bohemia](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Bohemia), the NLU sent some of their members to [Prehevil](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Prehevil), but communication was quickly lost with them. The organization decided to wait and see if their members would resurface, but Abella was not content to sit and wait. She boarded the first train to Prehevil, determined to find out what had happened, and was told that if she ever needed support, she would recognize the leader of the Bohemian branch of the NLU by her red shoes.

But unbeknownst to her, she would play a part in a greater scheme by the trickster Moon God, [[Rher]], as one of the fourteen Termina contestants.

*Source: [[Wiki - Abella]]*

### Personality

Abella is both a skilled and outgoing individual. She is willing to use her mechanical expertise to assist the party, though many of the horrors within Prehevil defy conventional solutions, which both puzzles and fascinates her.

Abella is quite perceptive, showing a great deal of knowledge and interest in the architecture of the various buildings in Prehevil. Her reasons for studying engineering and mechanics are that they always follow a clear logic. She also has somewhat of a motherly side, evident by her comments toward the abandoned houses and her mention that they could use some good housekeeping. Abella is optimistic and diplomatic with her fellow contestants. At times, she can seem rather light-hearted, cracking jokes in party talk to lighten the mood. At other times, she can be seen caring quite deeply for people such as [[Levi]], often showing sympathy and concern for his well-being.

Though she will happily work with the other contestants while exploring the city, Abella will intentionally hide her true identity as a resistance agent from them. She will, however, admit her connections with the NLU to a suspicious [[Karin]] and the player character if both contestants are brought to the speakeasy area of [PRHVL Bop](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/PRHVL_Bop).

*Source: [[Wiki - Abella]]*

### Gameplay traits

- Has a [Pipe wrench](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Pipe_wrench) and [Sturdy overalls](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Sturdy_overalls) equipped.
- Has a [Knife](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Knife), 4 [Shilling](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Shilling)s, and a [Lucky coin](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Lucky_coin_(F%26H2)) in her inventory (as main character).
- Has the Caressing soul, which unlocks the Caressing skill tree in the [Hexen](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Hexen_F%26H2).
- Can use two-handed weapons.
- Can use firearms.

##### Special event - Caligura at Mayor's Manor

Sleeping in the Mayor's Manor on Day 1 as Abella as the protagonist results in a cutscene where [[Caligura]] attempts to sexually assault her. This prompts a fight and an early in-game chance to dispatch Caligura if the player so wishes.

*Source: [[Wiki - Abella]]*

#### As a party member

If recruited, Abella will have [Sturdy overalls](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Sturdy_overalls) and a [Pipe wrench](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Pipe_wrench) equipped. She will also have the *[Wrench toss](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Wrench_toss)* and *[Short circuit](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Short_circuit)* skills learned. *Wrench toss* maintains its relevance throughout the entire game as a semi-reliable way to stun enemies, while *Short circuit* enables her to unlock electronic doors for the player as needed. Offering support in upcoming battles, enabling easier access to specific bunker areas, and being among the first characters available for recruitment position Abella as one of the most useful party members in the game.

*Source: [[Wiki - Abella]]*

### Trivia

- When playing as Abella, she will start off with a completely different outfit. When getting introduced to the others, Abella will change outfits to check up on the train.
- Playing as Abella and speaking to August outside the train at the start will confirm that they both come from the same region and provide unique dialogue about August's life story.
- Abella is the only character who can read the in-game notes written with passcodes. However, if other protagonists attempt to read those notes while she is in their party, Abella will take the note to translate it and say that those notes are illegible. This may imply that she is hiding the answer from the party on a particular subject.
- Abella can show up as alive on the train during Ending A, even if she has moonscorched.  ⚠ *(known bug listed on the wiki)*
- When creating Abella, game creator [Miro Haverinen](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Miro_Haverinen) found inspiration in female characters from Japanese role-playing games (JRPGs): her design draws elements from Karin Koenig from *Shadow Hearts: Covenant* and Aerith Gainsborough from *Final Fantasy VII*. Furthermore, her enemy battle sprite is modeled after the official artwork of Leila from *Final Fantasy II*.[^1]
  - Haverinen has also stated that Abella's initial concept featured her with blonde hair, sporting a yellow shirt paired with blue denim overalls — a departure from her final design.
- In a Discord poll put up by Haverinen on which character should be made available next, Abella came out as the most desired character to be made playable.
- Miro Haverinen has expressed dissatisfaction with his handling of Abella since the game's release. He believes that while there are a few lines in the game hinting at Abella's conflict, they remain quite rudimentary, and his original vision for the character is not adequately represented, prompting a desire to flesh out her storyline in a future update.[^2]
- Unused game data indicates that Abella was planned to have a [Doppelgänger](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Marina_(Doppelg%C3%A4nger)) counterpart.

*Source: [[Wiki - Abella]]*
