## Biography

> **''"What am I going to do when we finally escape all this? I'll probably start a small occult store somewhere. Somewhere FAR away from Prehevil... Maybe Rondon. I want to live somewhere busy. With tons of people around. People who wouldn't judge who I am or anyone else around them..."**

> - Marina''

**Marina Domek** is a Playable Character and a potential [Party Member](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Characters_List_F%26H2#Party_Members-0) in *[[Fear & Hunger 2 Termina|Fear & Hunger 2: Termina]]*.

An occultist that has refined her craft from a prestigious school, Marina focuses on the creative use of otherworldly powers, her abilities providing useful and unique buffs and effects. Marina is one of the fourteen contestants arriving in [Prehevil](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Prehevil) that were forced to participate in the [Festival of Termina](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Termina).

*Source: [[Wiki - Marina]]*

### Lore

Marina was born in the [Church of Alll-mer](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Church_of_Alll-mer) within [Prehevil](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Prehevil), where her lineage practiced archaic but brutal and cruel rites and rituals in order to appease the [[The Gods|old gods]]. Her father, [Father Domek](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Father_Domek), is the current head of the [Dark Priests](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Dark_Priests).

Her mother was not an active participant of these rituals, and did not want Marina to follow in her father's footsteps. The life of a dark priest is cruel, entailing a lifetime of grueling servitude to old gods - a life which her mother wished to spare her from. Therefore, despite being born a boy, Marina would be raised as a woman, effectively shielding her from the obligation of priesthood.

She would grow up homeschooled near the church and follow an orthodox path different from that of dark priests. Her skill and talent in occultism would soon be noticed by the Ministry of Darkness in the [Vatican](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Vatican_City), which offered her to study abroad - a scholarship of sorts to further her occult abilities. She eagerly accepted this opportunity to leave Prehevil, largely due to her father's scrutiny, judgment and apparent disdain for her very existence. Her mother, however, was proud of her. When departing from Prehevil, she chose to remain a woman, as she felt more comfortable that way, stating that it 'felt natural, so who cares'.

During her time in the [Vatican](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Vatican_City), she refined her abilities in occultism for a significant amount of time, feeling as though she spent a lifetime there when she returned to Prehevil. She had to go back to that forsaken place somewhere in the [Eastern Union](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Eastern_Union) after receiving a letter by her father, informing her of her mother's death. The circumstances surrounding her father's letter seemed suspicious, not only because the two did not share any sort of connection ever since Marina left for the Vatican, but also because his writing seemed very apathetic towards his wife's death. Marina suspected her father was involved - a suspicion fueled by her knowledge of her father's obsession with blood magic, whose rituals often demanded the blood of loved ones. She returned, determined to find out what happened to her family in Prehevil - secretly followed by a [[Samarie|certain someone]] without her knowledge.

Little did she know of the greater scheme that was playing out, which would lead to her becoming one of Termina's contestants...

*Source: [[Wiki - Marina]]*

### Personality

Marina is a curious young woman who speaks very informally. She often speaks her mind about any given situation. 

As a former resident of Prehevil, Marina is knowledgeable about various parts of the city, and will freely share what she knows with others. She has an intense distaste for her hometown, and does not seem disturbed by the disaster that has afflicted it.

Despite the resentment she holds for her father and his status as a dark priest, Marina will appear conflicted by his death, though she states she is glad his lineage will end.

*Source: [[Wiki - Marina]]*

### Trivia

- Marina's name, which is a feminine version of the Classical/Vulgar Latin name of Marinus, means *of the sea,* which comes from the Latin word, *Marinus*. In this context of name origin, Marina did go to the Vatican, which should be the fictional equivalent of Vatican City in Rome. The area is situated in the Italian Peninsula, which is surrounded by the Mediterranean Sea on its western, southern, and eastern flanks.
- When found in the Mayor's house she'll mention that it is a Tuesday, and will mention it is a Wednesday the day after in the [PRHVL Bop](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/PRHVL_Bop). There seems to be no other way to check the current date of the events other than this bit of dialogue.
- Marina is a transgender woman, and her identity as trans is occasionally brought up and plays a significant role in her story, mainly outside of the events of the [Termina](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Termina) festival. [Miro](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Miro_Haverinen) has furthermore enforced her identity in a comment thread on the game's itch.io page. [^1]
  - Her character intro states that she was raised by her mother as a girl, to protect her from the priesthood that was mandated for firstborn sons.
  - She also states in the intro that once growing up and leaving, she chose to retain that identity as she felt a lot more comfortable as a girl.
  - Her Moonscorched form represents her fears of her father imposing a masculine societal role upon her.
  - When [[Samarie]] encounters [Father Domek](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Father_Domek) in the cutscene before she kills him, he misgenders Marina at first, before switching back to she/her pronouns.
  - If Marina loses to [Father Oscar](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Decrepit_Priest) in Old Town, he will crucify her facing toward the cross, hiding her genitalia from the onlookers - likely out of some kind of disgust or contempt towards her identity.
  - Marina possesses the Changeling soul, the changeling being a mythological creature often characterized as a human-like shapeshifter.
- In the demo, using the soul stone on her if she died would reveal that she formerly had an Enlightened soul at one point in development, similar to the dark priest [[Enki]]. This was likely before the new souls to *Termina* and the lore surrounding them were implemented.
- When against her in battle, Marina's arms should have the ability to cast *Pyromancy Trick*, but in the game's files she has incorrectly been given the player's version of the skill, which requires 10 Mind points to cast. Her arms have a Max Mind of 0, meaning she is incapable of using the attack.

> [!warning] The wiki marks the following as speculation.

- The other person in Marina's B ending photograph bears a strong resemblance to [[Samarie]], though she is supposed to be dead in this route.
  - It could potentially be a Doppelganger of Samarie, since doppelgangers are known to mimic another person to replace them outside of the festival. There is also a chance that this person isn't [[Samarie]] at all.
  - Another possible explanation is that this is [[Samarie]]'s ghost, and her desire to be with Marina is so strong that her spirit stays near Marina after death. [Ghosts do exist](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Miner_Spectre) in the world of Fear and Hunger, with all characters able to see them, and Marina's training as an Occultist would increase her connection to them.
