# Ritual Circles F&H2

*This page explains the ritual circles in [[Fear & Hunger 2 Termina|Fear & Hunger 2: Termina]]. For the ritual circles in [[Fear & Hunger]], see [Ritual Circles F&H1](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Ritual_Circles_F%26H1).*

**Ritual circles** are a mechanic in *[[Fear & Hunger 2 Termina|Fear & Hunger 2: Termina]]*, which allows the player to benefit from various divine effects or to increase their affinity with different [[The Gods|Gods]]. The [Occult Grimoires](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Occult_Grimoires) book explains their usage.

The player has to draw the [sigil](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Sigil) of any given God in the circle to activate it, but it needs to be the right type; otherwise, the circle is wasted.

*Source: [[Wiki - Ritual Circles F&H2]]*

### How to use

For Asymmetric and Perfection Circles, the player will need the Skin Bible of each respective God in their inventory, as well as a piece of Chalk to draw on the circle. If the wrong symbol is drawn, nothing happens - it will also waste the circle, as it is not possible to erase it and try again. 

A matching symbol, when drawn by the player, will raise their affinity with that God and grant access to that God's skills in the [Hexen](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Hexen_F%26H2).

Imperfect Circles work differently, requiring the player to ritually offer specific items to interact with the New Gods.

*Source: [[Wiki - Ritual Circles F&H2]]*

#### Perfection Circle

This circle represents countless spiraling cycles in the birth of the Ascended Gods, the ones who were able to go beyond human limits.
- [[Alll-mer]]
  - Provides fast travel between two or more Alll-mer circles.
- [[God of Fear and Hunger]]
  - Provides one save point per statue without progressing time (three saves per statue on [Easy(er) mode](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Difficulty_Settings_F%26H2)).

*Source: [[Wiki - Ritual Circles F&H2]]*

#### Asymmetric Circle

This circle represents the Old Gods and their one-sided distribution of power.
- [[Gro-goroth]]
  - Sacrifice a team member for affinity, one-time use.
    - Trying to sacrifice a contestant will make them hostile and immediately start a fight.
    - A body charmed with *Necromancy* can be used as a safe sacrifice method.
  - *Blood Sacrifice* option from [[Samarie]]'s skill tree for gaining affinity.
    - This reduces the protagonist's Body Points to 1, who will need at least 50 Body Points to safely do it again for more affinity.
- [[Rher]]
  - Alternate realms, needed to speak to certain characters, access the [golden gates](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Golden_Gates), and/or obtain rust-colored pearls.
    - Mold 12 statues with [Black Kalev](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Black_Kalev) in the party to hear the Man in Black speak lore about Alll-mer.
- [[Sylvian]]
  - Full body parts restoration and full healing on body and mind for the whole party, recovers the sprinting ability, also cures Blindness (will not cure Heroin addiction).
  - *Masturbation* option from [[Samarie]]'s skill tree for affinity, one-time use.
- [[Vinushka]]
  - Instant tree growth, used to climb up or down to reach shortcuts or otherwise inaccessible areas, can also be used to provide specific items.

*Source: [[Wiki - Ritual Circles F&H2]]*

#### Imperfect Circle

This circle is the mark of the New Gods, who have strived for perfection since their conception, never to attain it truly.
- [[Heartless One]]
  - Offering the [Heart-shaped lock](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Heart-shaped_lock) will summon the Heartless One, initiating a fight. After defeating her, the player will receive the [Red virtue](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Red_virtue) and [The darkness](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/The_darkness) for equipment.
- [[Tainted One|The Tainted One]]
  - Requires the heads of enemies, which can be severed by interacting with their corpse using a bonesaw.
  - Each head will provide a [Soul stone shard](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Soul_stone_shard). Three are needed to form a [[Soul stone (F&H2)|Soul stone]], which can be used in the [Hexen](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Hexen_F%26H2). The recipe for Soul stone will be unlocked after offering heads.
- [[Tormented One (F&H2)|The Tormented One]]
  - Offering the [Dead crow](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Dead_crow) will summon the Tormented One, initiating a fight. Defeating him unlocks the *Chains of Torment* skill in [Hexen](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Hexen_F%26H2) for that run.
- [[Radiating One|The Radiating One]]
  - [Portrait of a young man](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Portrait_of_a_young_man) can be offered, which will spawn this unique vendor who will trade items for [Rust-coloured pearl](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Rust-coloured_pearl).
    - [8] [Leechmonger ring](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Leechmonger_ring)
    - [4] [Ring of Wraiths](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Ring_of_wraiths_(F%26H2))
    - [9] [Yggaegetsu amulet](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Yggaegetsu_amulet)
    - [4] [12-gauge Trenchgun](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/12-gauge_Trenchgun)
    - [10] [Mauler](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Mauler)

*Source: [[Wiki - Ritual Circles F&H2]]*

##### Asymmetric Circle

There are four asymmetric blank circles and five asymmetric circles with pre-made drawings, making a total of nine circles:
- [Outskirts of Prehevil - Broken Shack](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Outskirts_of_Prehevil_-_Broken_Shack) (one blank)
  - Vinushka: shortcut to [Deeper Woods](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Deeper_Woods), alternate route to [the city](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Prehevil).
  - Rher: leads to [Outskirts of Prehevil - Broken Shack (Rher Dimension)](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Outskirts_of_Prehevil_-_Broken_Shack_(Rher_Dimension)), which has seven clay that can be molded.
- [Abandoned House](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Abandoned_House) (one blank)
  - Vinushka: provides the [Pinecone Pig](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Pinecone_Pig_(F%26H2)), hanging on the left side of the tree on the first floor, which has a 33% chance to provide an item at the start of a battle against most enemies while equipped.
  - Rher: leads to [Abandoned House (Rher Dimension)](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Abandoned_House_(Rher_Dimension)), which has the man in black. Choose any option besides the third to recruit [Black Kalev](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Black_Kalev).
- [Mayor's Manor](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Mayor's_Manor) (one blank)
  - Vinushka: allows to climb down to the basement and collect the [Fluted fauld](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Fluted_fauld) (the basement can be climbed down to regardless of whether the sigil is drawn).  ⚠ *(known bug listed on the wiki)*
  - Rher: leads to [Mayor's Manor (Rher Dimension)](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Mayor's_Manor_(Rher_Dimension)), which has three [Ronteal](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Ronteal)s and a Golden Gate.
- [Tunnel 7](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Tunnel_7) (one premade Rher)
  - Premade Rher: leads to [Tunnel 7 (Rher Dimension)](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Tunnel_7_(Rher_Dimension)), which has four [Ronteal](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Ronteal)s and a Golden Gate. The exit leads outside the bunker entrance in the woods.
- [Orphanage](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Orphanage) (one premade Gro-goroth, Sylvian, and Rher) 
  - Premade Rher: leads to [Orphanage (Rher Dimension)](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Orphanage_(Rher_Dimension)), which has one of the effigies, five [Ronteal](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Ronteal)s, and a Golden Gate.
- [Church of Alll-mer](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Church_of_Alll-mer) basement (one blank, one premade Rher) 
  - Vinushka: leads to the roof and rafters, where a chest and [Sabbath](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Sabbath) can be found.
  - Rher: leads to [Church of Alll-mer (Rher Dimension 2)](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Church_of_Alll-mer_(Rher_Dimension_2)), which has a locked-up woman, need [Rusty key](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Rusty_key) and [Church keys](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Church_keys). The Rusty key can be found in the church basement if the chandelier is not dropped.
    - Unlocking the chained woman will lead to an optional 1v1 boss fight, [[Heartless One]], activated at an Imperfect Circle by sacrificing the Heart Shaped Lock given to the player.
  - Premade Rher: leads to [Church of Alll-mer (Rher Dimension 1)](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Church_of_Alll-mer_(Rher_Dimension_1)), which has [[Samarie]], five clay statues to mold, and a Golden Gate.

*Source: [[Wiki - Ritual Circles F&H2]]*
