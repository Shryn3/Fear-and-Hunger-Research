# Soul stone

*This page is about the miscellaneous consumable from [[Fear & Hunger]]. For the item of the same name from [[Fear & Hunger 2 Termina|Fear & Hunger 2: Termina]], see [[Soul stone (F&H2)]].*

The **Soul stone** is a [Miscellaneous Consumable](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Items_List_F%26H1#Miscellaneous_Consumables-0) found in *[[Fear & Hunger]]* and *[[Fear & Hunger 2 Termina|Fear & Hunger 2: Termina]]*. It is an important, mystical item that can mainly be exchanged for Skills, or used for other power increases.

*Source: [[Wiki - Soul stone]]*

### Gameplay

When a Soul stone is used on a normal enemy, it generates a Lesser soul, which can be used at the [[Hexen F&H1|Hexen Table]] to learn new [skills](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H1) or curse weapons. When used on certain unique enemies, it generates accessories that provide certain bonuses and status effects or unlocks a skill tree at the Hexen Table.

*Source: [[Wiki - Soul stone]]*

### Location

Here is the list of all Soul stones that can be found in the game:[^1]
- [[Enki]] and [[Ragnvaldr]] can start with Soul stones.
  - For Enki, choose "Stock up on magical items".
  - For Ragnvaldr, choose "Take souvenirs from Vinland".
- Level 1 Inner Courtyard: Kill one of the [dark priests](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Priest) and search the body.
- Level 3 Prison: Open the chest in one of the cells and win the coin flip.
- Level 4 Caverns: One is sitting near the [cave gnome](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Cavegnome) eggs. If you pick it up and go back towards the elevator, you will be attacked by the [Cave Mother](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Cavemother).
- Level 6 Mines, Cavedweller Village: Go into the connected houses, go up the ladder.
- Level 6 Mines, the other map: Open the chest near the Shark Teeth.
- Level 4 Thicket: Press the wall near Ragnvaldr, near where you drop in from Level 3 Thicket.
- Level 4 Thicket: Just past the [Cockroach King](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Cockroach_King), there's a place to fall to the next area. If you win the coin flip, you don't fall through the hole and can go to the next area, where a soul stone is on the floor.
- Level 5 Deeper Thicket: Go past the first [mumbler](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Mumbler) and go into the alcove along the top of the map.
- Level 7 Catacombs: Right after [Le'garde](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Le'garde)'s cell, in a chest. It's always a soul stone if you call the coinflip.
- Grand Library: Solve the Uterus puzzle and go down the ladder. A soul stone is on one of the platforms.
- Temple of Torment, Level 2: A Soul stone is sitting on the ground by a floor grate.
- Soul stones are sometimes dropped by [Yellow Mages](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Yellow_mage).
- Outside the Temple of Torment: Leave the Temple, make sure you're in Present [Ma'habre](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Ma'habre), and there will be a Soul stone sitting in front of a hole in the walkway.
- You can steal one Soul stone from the [Black Witch](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Black_Witch) or the [Body Snatcher](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Body_Snatcher), or two from [[Valteil]].
- Soul stones can be purchased from the [Bug-Eyed Figurine](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Bug-Eyed_Figurine) in Past Ma'habre for 20 coins.
- You can get a Soul stone using an [Empty Scroll](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Empty_Scroll).

*Source: [[Wiki - Soul stone]]*
