# Rev

**Rev** is a game mechanic introduced in *[[Fear & Hunger 2 Termina|Fear & Hunger 2: Termina]]*. It is a cumulative point system that can be used in battle to increase battle prowess.

*Source: [[Wiki - Rev]]*

### Gameplay

As combat progresses, party members gain 1 Rev point at the end of every turn, with a maximum of 3 points. "Revving up" boosts the damage of physical attacks; some [skills](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H2) also require revving up before being used. After combat ends, Rev points reset to zero and are not carried over to the next battle. Some skills provide Rev points, such as *[Meditation](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H2#Contestant_skills-0)*, which grants +2 Rev points when guarding, and *[Advanced Occultism](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H2#Contestant_skills-0)*, which gives +1 Rev point at the start of each battle, with *[Greater Occultism](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H2#Contestant_skills-0)* adding an additional +1 Rev point at the start of each battle. You can Rev up using PgDn and Rev down using PgUp; these controls can be customized in the settings.

*Source: [[Wiki - Rev]]*

#### Damage increase

- 1 Rev point increases physical damage by 30%
- 2 Rev points increases physical damage by 40%
- 3 Rev points increases physical damage by 40% and attacks twice.

*Source: [[Wiki - Rev]]*
