# Soul stone (F&H2)

**Soul stones** are [consumable items](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Items_List_F%26H2#Miscellaneous_Consumables-0) found in *[[Fear & Hunger 2 Termina|Fear & Hunger 2: Termina]]*, which can mainly be exchanged for skills, or used for other power increases.

*Source: [[Wiki - Soul stone (F&H2)]]*

### Gameplay

- Soul stones can be exchanged for new [skills](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H2) at the [Hexen](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Hexen_F%26H2) while dreaming, or on one of the Hexen tables found at [Donnovan's House](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Donnovan's_House) or at [St. Domek's Orphanage](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Orphanage) (note that the Orphanage table is not available in Fear & Hunger mode). They are most commonly obtained through crafting, as you can get many soul stone shards by trading the heads of enemies with the [[Tainted One]] at an [[Ritual Circles F&H2|Imperfect Ritual Circle]].

*Source: [[Wiki - Soul stone (F&H2)]]*

### Acquirement

- Can be crafted - Recipe: x3 [Soul stone shards](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Soul_stone_shard).
- [[O'saa]] starting choice.
- Drops from O'saa, [[Marina]], [Marina (Doppelgänger)](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Marina_(Doppelg%C3%A4nger)), [[Samarie]], [Decrepit Priest](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Decrepit_Priest), [Inquisitor](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Inquisitor), [Crimson Father](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Crimson_Father), and [Mastermind](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Mastermind).
- Can be [stolen](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Skills_List_F%26H2#Caligura_%28Decrepit_Soul%29) from O'saa, Marina, Marina (Doppelgänger), [Poe](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Poe) and Decrepit Priest.
- Can be bought for 50 [Shillings](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Shilling) in [Dr. Kefer's Tricks & Magic](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Dr._Kefer's_Tricks_%26_Magic) shop.
- Rare [Chest](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Loot_Tables_F%26H2#Chest) loot.
- Can be found in the Tes'tich table room in the [Orphanage](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Orphanage).
- Can be found at the back of [Dr. Kefer's Tricks & Magic](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Dr._Kefer's_Tricks_%26_Magic) shop inside of a lamp that will activate [Poe](https://fearandhunger.wiki.gg/wiki/Poe) encounters if looted.
