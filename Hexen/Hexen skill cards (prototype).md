# Hexen skill cards (prototype)

Prototype of the card layout for two sections only. Every field of the nine-column table is kept; empty fields are omitted. Needs `.obsidian/snippets/hexen-cards.css` enabled for the colours; without it the cards still render as plain collapsible callouts.

## Soul Blank

> [!hexen]- Diagnosis
> **F&H2 · Contestant skills · Daan**
>
> Study fallen enemies and their anatomy to gain knowledge of them. Diagnosis also reveals what strategies might work the best when facing similar foes in the future.
>
> **Effect:** Gives an option to examine defeated enemies in the overworld to find their weaknesses.
>
> **Cost:** Passive

> [!hexen]- Medicinal
> **F&H2 · Contestant skills · Daan**
>
> Sacrifice harvested organs to Vitruvia, the architect of the human body. A sacrifice will cure a corresponding status ailment. Medicinal is based on the law where every action has an equal reaction.
>
> **Effect:** Gives an option to use harvested organs to cure different status effects for 10 mind (Fracture requires only 7 mind): / Liver (Toxic) / Kidney (Poisoned) / Eye (Blindness) / Heart (Bleeding) / Parathyroid glands (Fracture) / Thymus (Infected arm/leg)
>
> **Cost:** Passive

> [!hexen]- Organ harvest
> **F&H2 · Contestant skills · Daan**
>
> Harvest organs from fallen foes to sacrifice them with your medicinal skills. The desecration of bodies used to be a taboo subject, but it is now widely used among medical practitioners.
>
> **Effect:** Gives an option to harvest organs from defeated enemies in the overworld.
>
> **Cost:** Passive

> [!hexen]- Precision stance
> **F&H2 · Contestant skills · Daan**
>
> A special stance to raise your accuracy greatly. Often used during medical surgeries to prevent one's arm from shaking.
>
> **Effect:** Applies Accuracy Boost status.
>
> **Cost:** 5 Mind

> [!hexen]- Magna-medicinal
> **F&H2 · Contestant skills · Daan**
>
> A greater form of medicinal where the surgeon will sacrifice their own limbs in exhange for human life. The procedure must be performed on the spot during an encounter to work properly.
>
> **Effect:** Gives an option to sacrifice a limb to revive a party member.
>
> **Cost:** 50 Mind / 1 limb

> [!hexen]- Analyze
> **F&H2 · Contestant skills · Daan**
>
> Study the anatomy of your opponent to quickly find the biggest weaknesses it their physique.
>
> **Effect:** Inflicts Weakness status to target's head.
>
> **Cost:** 20 Mind

> [!hexen]- Greater Analyze
> **F&H2 · Unused skills · Daan**
>
> Analyze the enemy behaviour and find the weak points. You can use the acquired information against similar type enemies.
>
> **Unlock:** Daan (Blank soul)

> [!hexen]- Reverse Healing
> **F&H2 · Unused skills · Daan**
>
> remove own organs to cause serious damage
>
> **Unlock:** Daan (Blank soul)

## Soul Caressing

> [!hexen]- Wrench toss
> **F&H2 · Contestant skills · Abella**
>
> Throw your pipe wrench at the enemy with devastating force. A chance of stun. Requires pipe wrench to be equipped first.
>
> **Effect:** Throws a Pipe wrench to deal 291-437 (Attack * 6 - 20 (20% variance)) Blunt damage with a 95% Chance to inflict Concussion status to one target. Damage affected by Rev. Pipe wrench is unequipped after use.
>
> **Cost:** Free

> [!hexen]- Short circuit
> **F&H2 · Contestant skills · Abella**
>
> Short circuit an electric lock to open locked doors. Relatively simple in practice, but short circuiting the wrong conductors might lock the door for good.
>
> **Effect:** Allows you to open doors with electronic panels in Tunnels.
>
> **Cost:** Passive

> [!hexen]- Trapcraft
> **F&H2 · Contestant skills · Abella**
>
> Craft bear traps and booby traps from different utility items. Used in the Crafting menu.
>
> **Effect:** Unlocks recipes for: - Bear trap - Booby trap
>
> **Cost:** Passive

> [!hexen]- Weaponcraft
> **F&H2 · Contestant skills · Abella**
>
> Use your imagination to craft unique weapons from different utility items. Used in the Crafting menu.
>
> **Effect:** Unlocks recipes for: - Sandman's kiss - Reaper's rake - Lantern flail - Meat grinder
>
> **Cost:** Passive

## God Alll-mer

> [!hexen]- Blood portal
> **F&H1 · God Affinity skills**
>
> A magic that enables one to create portals between ritual circles.
>
> **Effect:** Allows you to create portals on ritual circles and travel between them. Blood portal locations: - Level 1 - Entrance - Level 5 - Mines - Level 7 - Catacombs - Ancient city - Grand library - Ancient city - Tower of Endless
>
> **Cost:** Free
>
> **Unlock:** 1 Alll-mer affinity

> [!hexen]- Phase step
> **F&H1 · General skills**
>
> A world breaking magic that bends the space with the key [C]. Use at your own risk. It's very possible to end up stuck in a wall.
>
> **Effect:** Teleports the player 5 steps in the direction they are facing upon pressing [C].
>
> **Cost:** 10 Mind
>
> **Unlock:** Empty scroll with the phrase "PHASE"
